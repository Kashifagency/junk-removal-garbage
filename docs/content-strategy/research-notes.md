## 1. Competitive landscape
- **The niche is dominated by small exact-match-domain operators.** Examples: 800junk.ae, dubaijunkremovals.com, getmyjunkuae.com, removejunkservicedubai.com, junkremovalserviceindubai.com, dubaijunkremovalpick.com, gojunkuae.com, takedubaijunk.com, junksexpert.com.
- **Aggregators** (Justlife, ServiceMarket, Matic) did not appear for junk-removal terms in our searches (**UNVERIFIED** from Dubai). Check in a Dubai incognito session.
- **Long-tail terms look easy.** Spam `.it.com` sites and parasite posts rank for long-tail terms like "construction waste removal dubai" and "junk removal dubai marina".
- **Strongest informational competitors** are property portals:
  - Property Finder — "Junk Removal Services in Dubai: Full Guide" (Nov 2025, https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/junk-removal-services/) and "Bulk Waste Disposal in Dubai" (Feb 2026, https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/?p=199499)
  - Dubizzle — https://www.dubizzle.com/blog/property/dubai-municipality-bulk-waste-disposal-service/
  - Bayut — https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/how-recycle-electronic-waste-dubai/
- **Competitor gaps we can exploit:**
  - Few real area landing pages (areas are listed as plain text).
  - Empty blogs, e.g. dubaijunkremovals.com shows "No Content Available".
  - No Arabic versions (800junk.ae, dubaijunkremovals.com).
- **Common competitor angles:** "free removal" of reusable items while charging for waste, same-day service, WhatsApp photo pricing, fixed prices with "no stair fees", recycling claims, trust stats (years in business, number of pickups, Google rating).

## 2. Dubai Municipality free bulky waste service
- **What it is:** free, covering used household furniture, electrical appliances and electronics.
- **Who can't apply:** residents of real estate development zones and free zones.
- **Context:** 3 field teams working Sunday to Friday mornings; about 4,000 requests in the first half of 2022. Source: https://www.dm.gov.ae/dubai-municipality-promotes-waste-segregation-through-free-bulky-waste-disposal-service/ (12 Jul 2022)
- **Exclusions:** home maintenance waste, agricultural waste, sinks and toilets. Source: DM Arabic bulky-waste page (via search snippet).
- **How to request:** WhatsApp **800900**. DM schedules within 3 days and confirms by SMS. Source: https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/dubai-offers-free-bulk-waste-removal-service-via-whatsapp (24 Apr 2024)
- **Not confirmed by official sources** (Property Finder, Feb 2026):
  - requests also work via the DM app, DubaiNow and the website
  - items must be at ground level, and crews don't carry items down from high-rises
  - the excluded areas include Palm Jumeirah and Discovery Gardens
- **Demand:** 9,696 requests by end of October 2023 vs 7,782 the year before (+19.7%). Source: https://www.emaratalyoum.com/local-section/other/2023-11-20-1.1801924/
- **Why it matters:** freehold and development-zone communities, high-rises, renovation waste and urgent jobs fall outside the free service. That is the private-service market.

## 3. Fines and law (verify exact amounts before publishing)
- **Executive Council Resolution No. 14 of 2015** sets fines for dumping waste in public places, illegal hazardous waste disposal and construction debris outside approved sites (amounts per the search summary; check the schedule). https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2015/Executive%20Council%20Resolution%20No.%20(14)%20of%202015.html

## 4. Construction and demolition waste
- **On-site sorting:** DM Circular 12-10-1 (2023) requires C&D waste to be sorted on site. https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2024/12/12-10-1.pdf
  - **Verified 10 Oct 2026 (English technical translation):** dated 7 June 2023, addressed to engineering consultants and construction and demolition contractors. Requirements: a barriered area or separate containers per material (concrete, rebar, wood, tiles, plastics), with materials not mixed in transport. On demolition sites, separate concrete and rebar and remove doors, windows, aluminium, cables and metal sections first. Contractors keep records of quantities sorted and transported, using forms on the Dubai Building Permit and Control System. The consultant supervises compliance.
- **Licence activity:** DM Circular 6/2015 requires C&D haulers to hold a specific DM activity on their trade licence. https://www.dm.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/CIRCULAR-6-2015خدمات-جمع-ونقل-مخلفات-البناء-والهدم.pdf
  - **Owner action:** confirm whether the business licence includes this activity. If it does, it's a strong trust claim for the construction page.
- **Disposal fees:** set by Executive Council Resolution No. 58 of 2017.
- **Waste Destruction Certificate:** AED 250 (DM Technical Guideline No. 10).
- **Note:** Tadweer is Abu Dhabi's waste authority, so don't cite it for Dubai rules.
