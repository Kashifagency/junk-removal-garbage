This guide explains what to separate, how villa access affects the job and what details help us plan a tidy collection.

## How does garden waste removal in Dubai work?

**Send photos of the complete outdoor pile, tell us where it is and describe the access route.** We review the volume, item types and carrying distance, then confirm clear pricing before work starts. Same-day or next-day service is available subject to the schedule and community access.

A useful photo set includes one wide image of the whole pile and closer views of heavy or awkward items. Add your area, the nearest gate, whether a truck can stop near the property, and whether the crew must carry material through the house. These details matter more than a rough number of bags.

On collection day, keep pets and children away from the loading route. The crew carries the agreed materials out, loads them and leaves the immediate pickup area clear. Materials are handled responsibly, with recycling used whenever possible.

> **Tip:** Do not move the pile into a shared road, pavement or community space before the collection is confirmed. Keep it inside the property and ask community management if outside access or a contractor entry is required.

## Which garden materials can be collected?

**Common collections include plant cuttings and non-hazardous items left after gardening or landscaping.** Tell us if the load contains anything other than green material so it can be assessed correctly.

Typical items include:

- Palm fronds and trimmed branches
- Hedge cuttings, leaves and grass clippings
- Small logs and non-treated timber from garden work
- Bagged weeds and plant material
- Limited quantities of loose soil or sand, disclosed in advance
- Empty plant pots and damaged planters
- Old garden chairs, tables and outdoor storage items
- Cardboard and packaging from new garden furniture

Leaves take up space but weigh relatively little. Wet soil, filled planters and thick timber are far heavier, so they can change the vehicle, crew and loading time. Photograph those items separately rather than placing them behind bags where they cannot be seen.

If the property also has furniture, appliances or indoor clutter, show the complete job so it can be planned as one collection rather than two incomplete visits.

## How should you prepare branches, leaves and clippings?

**Group similar materials and contain small loose waste without making bags dangerously heavy.** Preparation is not about doing the crew's lifting; it is about keeping sharp, loose and heavy materials visible.

| Material | Helpful preparation | What to tell us |
|---|---|---|
| Leaves and grass | Use sturdy bags and keep them dry where possible | Approximate bag count |
| Palm fronds | Stack in one direction away from paths | Length and pile size |
| Branches | Group loose branches; do not conceal sharp ends | Thickest branch and access width |
| Soil and sand | Use manageable containers, not overloaded bags | Quantity and whether it is wet |
| Pots and planters | Empty water and identify cracked pieces | Size, material and whether soil remains |
| Outdoor furniture | Clear personal items from drawers or storage boxes | Dimensions and whether dismantling is needed |

Avoid overfilling plastic sacks with soil. A bag that can be lifted when dry may split or become unsafe once wet. If branches contain thorns, warn the crew and keep them separate from soft bags.

## What must stay out of a garden waste pile?

**Chemicals and hazardous materials must not be mixed with branches, leaves or ordinary garden clutter.** Pesticides, fertilisers, fuel, paint, solvents and unknown liquids need an appropriate specialist route. We also do not take medical waste.

Keep broken glass, sharp metal and electrical items visible and separate. A bag labelled as leaves should not contain hidden bottles, blades or contaminated containers. If a gardener or landscaping contractor has left an unlabelled product, ask them what it is before anyone moves it.

Dubai heat also matters. Do not leave oily rags, fuel containers, batteries or pressurised cans in direct sun beside dry green waste. Move people away from a leaking container and seek the correct specialist advice rather than adding it to a general pickup.

## What affects garden waste collection pricing?

**Volume, weight, access and the mix of materials determine the work involved.** A row of light leaf bags near a driveway is different from wet soil, concrete planters and branches carried from a rear garden.

The main pricing factors are:

- Total volume and whether more than one load may be needed
- Heavy materials such as soil, sand, stone-filled pots or thick timber
- Distance from the pile to the vehicle
- Steps, narrow side gates and routes through the home
- Dismantling needed for outdoor furniture or storage units
- Same-day timing and community access restrictions

Send one group photo plus close-ups of the largest pieces. If a side entrance is narrow, include a photo or its approximate width. We confirm pricing before work starts rather than publishing one figure that cannot reflect every garden.

Heavy planters may need a service lift and a trolley route even when the waste volume looks small.
