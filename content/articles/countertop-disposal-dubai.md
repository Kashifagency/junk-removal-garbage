## What affects the cost of countertop disposal in Dubai

Countertop removal costs reflect the physical dimensions, material weight, and accessibility of your property:

- **Material type and thickness:** A lightweight postformed laminate counter is far simpler to carry and load than a 30mm thick solid granite or quartzite island slab weighing hundreds of kilograms.
- **Total length and number of pieces:** Clearing a compact 2-metre straight kitchen run requires fewer crew members than removing a complete U-shaped kitchen layout with waterfall island panels and matching bathroom vanity counters.
- **Slab condition (intact extraction vs sectioned):** If a slab must be preserved intact for careful transport, additional lifting manpower and rigging are required. If the material is destined for crushing and can be sectioned, extraction is faster and more economical.
- **Service lift availability vs stair carries:** Direct access from your apartment door to an adjacent freight elevator and loading dock ensures rapid loading. Hauling dense stone down multiple flights of stairs in low-rise residential buildings requires extra crew members and specialized stair-climbing gear.

## Dubai Municipality regulations and stone recycling

Disposing of stone, composite mineral materials, and renovation joinery in Dubai is subject to strict environmental oversight under municipal law:

### Compliance with Circular 12-10-1 and Law No. 18 of 2024

Under Dubai Municipality Circular 12-10-1 regarding construction and demolition waste management, heavy mineral masonry, broken stone slabs, and renovation debris cannot be mixed into domestic garbage bins. Discarding dense stone worktops in communal residential bins breaches Law No. 18 of 2024 on public cleanliness, attracting substantial municipal fines starting from AED 500 up to AED 10,000. Our operations comply fully with all municipal waste standards, safeguarding property owners and tenants from regulatory penalties.

### Mineral aggregate crushing and material recovery in Al Quoz

Rather than taking up scarce space in municipal landfill sites, all collected granite, marble, and quartz countertops are routed directly to licensed recycling facilities in Al Quoz and Dubai Industrial City. Massive aggregate crushers reduce clean natural stone into graded road-base aggregate and concrete ballast for regional infrastructure projects. Meanwhile, metal sink brackets, screws, and stainless steel fixings are separated for regional ferrous scrap reclamation.
