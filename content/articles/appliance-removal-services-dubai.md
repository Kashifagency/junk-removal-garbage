Upgrading the fridge, replacing a broken washing machine, or clearing out an old AC unit after a renovation? **Appliance removal in Dubai** is a job most people can't do alone. Appliances are heavy, awkward to carry through doorways and lifts, and full of metal, plastic and electronic parts that shouldn't end up in a bin room. This guide covers which appliances we take, how to prepare them, what affects the price, and how to book a quick collection.

## How does appliance removal work in Dubai?

Getting rid of an old appliance with us takes four steps:

1. **Send a photo** of the appliance on WhatsApp, with your area and floor number.
2. **Know the price before we start**, based on the type of appliance, how many items there are and the access.
3. **We carry it out.** Our crew uses dollies and straps to move heavy units safely from your kitchen, laundry area or balcony to our truck.
4. **Responsible disposal.** Metal, plastic and electronic parts are recycled whenever possible.

You don't need to move anything to the corridor or the loading bay first. We collect from where the appliance is.

## How to prepare appliances for collection

A little preparation makes collection safer and quicker:

- **Fridges and freezers:** empty them completely, unplug them and let them defrost for around 24 hours. Wipe out any water so nothing leaks in lifts or corridors.
- **Washing machines and dishwashers:** turn off the water supply, disconnect and drain the hoses, and unplug the machine. If it's plumbed in and you're unsure, ask your maintenance company to disconnect it first.
- **AC units:** installed split and window units must be safely uninstalled first, usually by an AC technician or your building's maintenance team. Once they're disconnected, we remove them.
- **Ovens and cookers:** make sure gas or electrical connections have been safely disconnected by a qualified person before collection.

> **Important:** For safety, we don't disconnect gas supplies or electrical wiring. Please arrange that beforehand, and tell us when you book if anything is still connected.

## Why appliances shouldn't be dumped

Fridges, freezers and AC units contain refrigerant gases and compressors. Washing machines and ovens are mostly metal, and almost every appliance contains electronic parts. All of that makes appliances worth recycling and unsuitable for normal waste. Dubai has licensed e-waste recycling capacity: Enviroserve in Dubai Industrial City handles electronic waste, [according to Bayut](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/how-recycle-electronic-waste-dubai/).

Leaving an old fridge or washing machine beside the bins or on the street can also break building rules, and dumping waste in public places is fined under Dubai's waste regulations. A proper collection keeps you on the right side of both.

## Moving out? Check who owns the appliances first

Many rented apartments and villas in Dubai come with appliances supplied by the landlord, such as the fridge, washing machine, oven and AC units. Before booking removal at the end of a tenancy:

- **Check your tenancy contract or inventory** to confirm which appliances belong to you and which belong to the landlord.
- **Only dispose of items you own.** If a landlord-owned appliance is broken, raise it with your landlord or agent rather than removing it.
- **Photograph the kitchen and laundry area** after your own appliances have gone, so the handover condition is clear.

If you're clearing the whole property before handover, our [household junk removal](/services/household-junk-removal/) service can take furniture, appliances and general junk in one visit.

## Can Dubai Municipality take my old appliance?

Dubai Municipality's [free bulky waste service](https://www.dm.gov.ae/dubai-municipality-promotes-waste-segregation-through-free-bulky-waste-disposal-service/) covers electrical appliances and electronics for eligible residents, and requests are made via WhatsApp on 800900. Residents of real estate development zones and free zones can't apply, and collections are scheduled after your request. If you're not eligible, need the appliance carried out from inside your home, or need it gone quickly, private appliance removal is the faster option.
