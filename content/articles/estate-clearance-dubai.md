Clearing a parent's apartment or a relative's villa is one of the hardest jobs a family faces, and in Dubai it nearly always comes with a deadline: a tenancy ending, a property sale, or relatives who have to fly home. **Estate clearance in Dubai** is our service for exactly this situation. We empty the home room by room, leave untouched anything the family is keeping, and take the rest away for recycling or proper disposal.

This guide explains how an estate clearance works here, what to decide before the crew arrives, and how families living abroad can arrange it remotely. It's part of our [household junk removal service](/services/household-junk-removal/), which covers apartments and villas across Dubai.

## What is estate clearance in Dubai, and when do families need it?

An estate clearance is the full removal of a home's contents after a death, a move into care or a permanent move away. It differs from a normal [house clearance in Dubai](/house-cleanout-dubai-complete-home-clearing-service/) mainly in pace and care: there are often personal papers, photographs and keepsakes mixed in with everyday furniture.

Families usually call us in one of these situations:

- **A tenancy is ending.** The landlord needs the apartment or villa back empty, often within a set notice period.
- **The property is being sold or handed back.** Agents and buyers expect vacant possession.
- **A relative has moved into care or overseas.** Some furniture follows them, and the rest has to go.
- **The family lives abroad.** Nobody can stay in Dubai for the weeks a DIY clearance takes.

## How does an estate clearance work?

The process is the same four steps we use for every household job, with extra time set aside for sorting.

1. **Send photos or a video on WhatsApp.** Walk through each room, including store rooms, balconies, maid's rooms and the garden. Tell us the building or community and the floor.
2. **Agree the price and the keep list.** Pricing is clear before work starts. You tell us which rooms, cupboards or items stay untouched.
3. **We carry everything out.** The crew dismantles beds and wardrobes, wraps glass and mirrors, and takes everything down by the service lift or out to the truck.
4. **Sorting and responsible disposal.** Materials are recycled whenever possible, and the rest goes to approved disposal sites.

> **Tip:** Put a coloured sticker or tape on anything the family is keeping, and gather those items in one room if you can. A single "keep room" with the door closed is the clearest instruction a crew can get.

## What should the family do before collection day?

The most useful thing you can do is decide what stays. Once items are on the truck they are mixed with other waste and can't be recovered.

### Search for documents and valuables first

Go through drawers, wardrobe shelves, handbags, coat pockets and the backs of cupboards for:

- passports, Emirates ID, bank cards and chequebooks
- property papers, tenancy contracts and insurance documents
- jewellery, watches, cash and keys
- photo albums, letters, USB drives and phones

If time is short, box up all paperwork unread and go through it later. It's far easier than sorting it under pressure on collection day.

### Wipe or remove personal data

Laptops, phones, tablets and external drives hold personal accounts and photographs. Take them with you or wipe them before the clearance. Read our [e-waste disposal guide](/e-waste-disposal-dubai/) for what to do with old electronics.

### Arrange access with building management

Most Dubai towers need a service lift booking, and some ask for a move-out or contractor permit. Villa communities may need the crew's details at the gate. Book these early, because a missed lift slot can push the whole job to another day.

## What affects the price of an estate clearance?

We don't publish fixed prices because no two homes are alike. These are the factors that decide the price:

- **Volume:** how many rooms, and how full they are
- **Item types:** heavy wardrobes, pianos, safes or many appliances need more crew time

- **Sorting time:** a clearance that needs careful room-by-room checks takes longer than a straight removal

## How do apartments and villas differ for estate clearance?

**Apartments** are mostly about lift access. In towers in areas such as [Dubai Marina](/dubai-marina/), the crew works around the building's service lift booking, protects corridor floors and lift walls, and loads at the designated loading bay. Large wardrobes are usually dismantled to fit the lift.

**Villas** usually have more space and more stored items: store rooms under the stairs, garages, rooftop rooms and gardens. In communities such as [Arabian Ranches](/arabian-ranches/), the truck can often park close to the door. The challenge is volume rather than access, and a full villa can need more than one truck load.

In summer, ask for an early start when you book. It keeps the heaviest outdoor carrying out of the midday heat, especially on villa jobs.

## Can Dubai Municipality's free bulky waste service help?

Partly. Dubai Municipality runs a free bulky waste collection for used household furniture and appliances, which you can request through WhatsApp on 800900. According to [Dubai Municipality](https://www.dm.gov.ae/dubai-municipality-promotes-waste-segregation-through-free-bulky-waste-disposal-service/), residents of real estate development zones and free zones are not eligible, and the service doesn't cover a full home clearance with sorting and carrying from every room. Check the current conditions before you rely on it.

For a whole home on a deadline, a private crew that clears everything in one visit is usually the practical choice.
