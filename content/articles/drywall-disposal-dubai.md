## Navigating Dubai Municipality regulations and building management rules

Disposing of gypsum board waste in Dubai is tightly regulated to protect the environment and maintain urban infrastructure:

### Dubai Municipality waste segregation rules

Under Dubai Municipality Law No. 18 of 2024 Regulating Waste Management and Administrative Resolution No. 34 of 2026, construction and demolition waste generators must ensure materials are separated and transferred through authorized waste transportation companies. Specifically, Dubai Municipality Circular 12-10-1 (2023) mandates that building materials be sorted on site to prevent cross-contamination.

Gypsum is primarily composed of calcium sulphate dihydrate. When dumped improperly into moist municipal landfills alongside organic waste, anaerobic bacteria decompose the sulphate, producing foul-smelling and toxic hydrogen sulphide gas. Consequently, Dubai Municipality requires clean gypsum waste to be segregated and diverted to specialized recycling plants that pulverize plasterboard for soil treatment or cement manufacturing additives.

Dumping construction debris in open desert plots, vacant plots in residential neighbourhoods, or around public road skips is strictly illegal. Municipal penalties under Law No. 18 of 2024 range from AED 10,000 to AED 100,000 for non-compliant dumping. Our commercial transport fleet holds all required municipal permits to collect, transport, and dispose of drywall legitimately (review official guidelines in our [construction waste disposal rules in Dubai](/construction-waste-disposal-rules-dubai/)).

## Sustainable recycling of gypsum board and metal studs

Reducing construction waste sent to landfills is an operational priority. Gypsum plasterboard is virtually 100% recyclable when kept free from heavy chemical contamination:

- **Paper and gypsum separation:** At licensed recycling facilities in Al Quoz, heavy mechanical crushers and vibrating screens separate the exterior cellulose paper facings from the chalky gypsum core.
- **Gypsum pulverization:** The reclaimed mineral gypsum is ground into fine powder. It is supplied to local cement factories as a setting retarder or processed for agricultural soil conditioning to improve moisture retention in arid sandy soil.
- **Paper pulp recycling:** The outer paper liners are baled and sent to industrial paper mills for pulp reprocessing into cardboard packaging.
- **Ferrous metal reclamation:** Steel framing studs, runner tracks, and screws are sorted using electromagnetic belts and forwarded to domestic steel mills for melting and structural re-rolling.
