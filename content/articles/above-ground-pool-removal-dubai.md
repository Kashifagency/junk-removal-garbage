### Dealing with UV degradation and seized pool frames

Dubai's intense desert sunlight accelerates plastic and polymer degradation at an astonishing rate. Over two or three summer seasons, UV radiation leaches plasticisers from flexible vinyl pool walls, transforming pliable liners into brittle, razor-sharp sheets that crack under pressure. 

Furthermore, mineral-dense tap water combined with chlorine accelerates galvanic corrosion where metal support pins insert into galvanized steel uprights. Homeowners attempting to pull these joints apart by hand often find the metal pipes completely fused. Our removal crews carry specialised penetrating lubricants, rubber mallets, and reciprocating mechanical saws to slice through seized connectors safely without jarring boundary walls or damaging neighbouring patio tiles.

### Safe disposal of filter sand and media

A standard residential above-ground pool sand filter contains between 25 kg and 100 kg of dense silica sand or recycled crushed glass filter media. Dumping wet filter sand into residential stormwater drains or sewer cleanouts is strictly prohibited across Dubai communities because it rapidly settles, causing severe subterranean pipe blockages. 

Our team manually scoops out wet filter sand into heavy-duty woven polypropylene rubble sacks. We haul the sand away to licensed aggregate and soil recovery depots, ensuring your domestic plumbing and drainage infrastructure remain completely protected.
