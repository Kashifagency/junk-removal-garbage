## Navigating Dubai mall rules: after-hours permits and loading docks

Conducting commercial retail work in premier UAE shopping destinations requires meticulous adherence to private landlord protocols and health and safety requirements.

### Night-shift operations and mall trading curfews

Major Dubai malls, including Mall of the Emirates, Dubai Mall, and city-centre retail hubs, strictly prohibit contractor movements, waste transport, or noisy dismantling while shops are open to the public. All shop clearance tasks must occur during the mall fit-out and de-fit window, commonly starting at 11:00 PM and concluding by 6:00 AM. Our teams are fully equipped for overnight shifts, arriving promptly at the scheduled dock time to complete the clearance before morning retail staff arrive.

### Mall delivery bay access and security gate passes

Entering a shopping centre service basement requires prior registration. Mall facilities management requires contractor submissions detailing vehicle registration numbers, driver details, and Emirates IDs at least 24 to 48 hours in advance. We supply all required driver credentials and commercial vehicle documentation promptly, ensuring smooth security check-in at the security gatehouse.

### Protecting mall finishes and service corridors

Mall management inspects common service corridors, terrazzo floors, and elevator interiors before and after contractor clearing shifts. If a contractor scrapes a wall or chips hallway tiles, repair deductions are made against the tenant's security deposit. Our crews use rubber-wheeled trolleys, protective corner sleeves, and moving blankets to ensure zero damage along service corridors.

## Free municipal bulky waste vs private commercial shop clearance

Tenants and shop managers sometimes wonder whether Dubai Municipality's free bulky waste service can clear store furniture.

Dubai Municipality provides a free bulky waste collection programme (requested via WhatsApp 800900) exclusively for eligible residential households. It strictly excludes commercial enterprises, private retail shops, shopping mall units, and business developments. Furthermore, municipal crews do not dismantle commercial fixtures, enter mall service bays, or work overnight shifts. For commercial retail spaces, hiring a licensed private junk removal and clearance specialist is the only legally compliant option under Dubai commercial waste regulations.
