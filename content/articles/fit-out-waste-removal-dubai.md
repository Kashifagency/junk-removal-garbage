## Regulatory compliance: Dubai Municipality Circular 12-10-1

The Dubai government enforces strict environmental standards for construction and interior fit-out projects. Compliance is strictly monitored by Dubai Municipality inspectors and third-party engineering consultants.

Under **Dubai Municipality Circular 12-10-1 (2023)**, commercial fit-out contractors and waste management haulers must segregate demolition waste directly on site into designated material streams:
- Structural metal framing and cabling must be separated from gypsum drywall panels.
- Clean timber offcuts and wooden pallets must not be co-mingled with concrete rubble or broken ceramic tiles.
- Mixed dumping of unsegregated debris into general municipal waste containers is strictly prohibited.

Furthermore, under [Executive Council Resolution No. 14 of 2015](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2015/Executive%20Council%20Resolution%20No.%20(14)%20of%202015.html) and [Law No. 18 of 2024 Regulating Waste Management](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2024/Law%20No.%20(18)%20of%202024%20Regulating%20Waste%20Management.html), illegal dumping of commercial or construction debris on vacant land carries fines starting from AED 5,000 up to AED 50,000, escalating dramatically for repeated commercial violations.

Our commercial operations operate under approved municipal licensing activities, ensuring that all collected materials are processed through accredited circular economy channels.
