However, when a commercial lease concludes, industrial tenancy agreements and property management guidelines mandate that all tenant-installed intermediate floors, steel catwalks, and access stairs must be completely removed to reinstate the building back to its original bare warehouse shell.

Dismantling an industrial mezzanine platform in Dubai involves serious engineering, safety, and logistical challenges. Structural mezzanine floors consist of heavy universal steel columns, primary crossbeams, interlocking cold-rolled steel purlins, and dense timber or checkered steel decking. These assemblies weigh anywhere from 2 to 20 metric tons and are bolted firmly into the reinforced concrete slab. Mishandling heavy steel sections during deconstruction can buckle surrounding walls, damage warehouse roller doors, or crack the concrete floor. Furthermore, industrial estate authorities require strict compliance with Dubai Civil Defence fire egress codes and Dubai Municipality waste segregation directives.

## What affects the cost of mezzanine floor removal in Dubai

Mezzanine deconstruction pricing in Dubai depends on structural specifications and site accessibility:

- **Platform square meterage and steel tonnage:** Dismantling a compact 30-square-meter office mezzanine involves far less material and crane time than taking down a multi-tier 500-square-meter industrial distribution platform weighing several tons.
- **Decking construction:** Lightweight interlocking plywood or tongue-and-groove chipboard unscrews quickly. Welded steel grating or heavy durbar plates requiring extensive bolt cutting and multi-man lifting increases on-site labour hours.

- **Warehouse door access:** Warehouses with wide drive-in roller shutter bays permit our flatbed trucks and lifting equipment to enter directly beneath the mezzanine. Facilities requiring long manual carrying runs through narrow pedestrian doors involve additional handling.

Mixing heavy carbon steel with general commercial refuse or discarding metal offcuts in communal bins is strictly prohibited and subject to severe penalties.
