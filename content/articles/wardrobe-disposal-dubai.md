## Why do wardrobes almost always require dismantling in Dubai?

Unlike single-family homes with large ground-floor double doors, most Dubai residents live in multi-story apartment towers or townhouses. Dismantling is almost always mandatory for several reasons:

### Standard door and ceiling heights
A typical assembled wardrobe stands between 2.0 and 2.4 meters high, with depths of 60 to 70 centimeters. Standard interior bedroom doorways in developments across [Jumeirah Village Circle](/jumeirah-village-circle/) and [Downtown Dubai](/downtown-dubai/) are typically 2.0 meters high and 80 centimeters wide. Maneuvering an assembled wardrobe through these openings—especially when hallway corners turn sharply—is physically impossible.

### Service lift and elevator dimensions
Even when an apartment tower has a dedicated service lift, the interior cab height or door clearance rarely fits an intact double or triple wardrobe. Attempting to force an assembled unit into a lift can scratch stainless steel walls or trigger elevator safety sensors, leading to costly building management penalties.

### Protecting walls for tenancy security deposit refunds
During move-out inspections, Dubai landlords and property management agencies inspect hallway walls, door jambs, and skirting boards for gouges or paint chips. Carrying heavy, rigid furniture corners through tight corridors risks scratching paintwork. Taking the unit apart into flat panels guarantees smooth, damage-free egress.

## Responsible recycling and material disposal

We take environmental sustainability seriously. Old furniture dumped in open desert areas or beside community trash enclosures damages the local environment and incurs heavy municipal fines under Dubai waste management laws.

When our crew collects your old wardrobe:
- **Clean timber and wood panels:** Unpainted wood and particleboard components are delivered to local recycling centers for reprocessing into composite board or mulch.
- **Metal hardware:** Handles, steel hinges, drawer runners, and hanging rods are separated for metal recycling.
- **Glass and mirrors:** Glass panels are safely handled and delivered to specialized recyclers where feasible.
- **Residual waste:** Any non-recyclable composite adhesives or worn plastic fittings are disposed of at licensed municipal waste treatment facilities.
