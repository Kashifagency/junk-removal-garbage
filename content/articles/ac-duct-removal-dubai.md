## Dubai Municipality standards and sheet metal recycling

Disposing of mechanical scrap and insulation in Dubai is strictly regulated under municipal environmental directives:

### Compliance with Circular 12-10-1 and Law No. 18 of 2024

Under Dubai Municipality Circular 12-10-1 regarding construction and demolition waste segregation, metal scrap, ducting, and building materials must be sorted at the source and diverted from general waste landfills. Discarding commercial sheet metal or fiberglass insulation in communal skips breaches Law No. 18 of 2024 on public cleanliness, resulting in substantial municipal fines starting from AED 500 up to AED 10,000. Our operations comply fully with all municipal waste regulations, safeguarding commercial tenants and contractors from legal liabilities.

### Metal reclamation and industrial recycling in Al Quoz

Rather than filling regional landfill sites, 100% of collected galvanised steel ducts, aluminium flex conduits, and steel unistrut hangers are routed directly to licensed metal recycling smelters in Al Quoz and Dubai Industrial City. Magnetic sorting systems separate ferrous steel from non-ferrous aluminium, melting the metals down to manufacture new construction rebar and industrial sheet coils, directly advancing Dubai's circular economy.
