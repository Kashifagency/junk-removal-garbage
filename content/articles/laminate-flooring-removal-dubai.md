## Dubai Municipality waste standards and wood recycling

Disposing of interior renovation joinery and composite wood boards in Dubai is strictly regulated by municipal environmental frameworks:

### Compliance with Circular 12-10-1

Under Dubai Municipality Circular 12-10-1 regarding construction and demolition waste segregation, composite timber, HDF boards, and demolition plastics cannot be mixed into general waste streams. Building contractors and property occupants are legally obligated to sort recyclable materials at the source. Our collection process ensures that HDF laminate cores, real wood veneers, and aluminium threshold profiles are separated into clean material streams before leaving your premises.

### Al Quoz material recovery and biomass processing

Rather than sending valuable composite wood to municipal landfills, all collected laminate flooring and clean timber offcuts are routed to licensed recycling centers in Al Quoz and Dubai Industrial City. Unlaminated and raw wood components are chipped into landscape mulch or compressed into industrial wood pellets, while metal transitions and screws are sorted into regional ferrous scrap recycling.
