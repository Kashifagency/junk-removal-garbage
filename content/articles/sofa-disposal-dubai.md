1. **Send a photo.** WhatsApp a picture of the sofa (and anything else that needs to go) with your area and floor number.

Larger jobs, such as a full living room or several rooms, are planned with the right crew size so everything goes in one visit.

## How to prepare your sofa for collection

A few minutes of preparation makes collection day faster and keeps the price accurate:

- **Check under the cushions.** Remove keys, remotes, coins and anything else that has slipped down the sides. Once the sofa is on the truck, it's gone.
- **Take off removable covers and throws** you want to keep.
- **Clear the route out.** Move rugs, side tables and anything fragile from the path between the sofa and the front door.
- **Measure tight spots.** Note the width of doorways, corridors and the service lift door, and share them with us if the sofa is large.
- **Separate what stays.** If you're keeping matching armchairs or footstools, put them in another room so nothing goes by mistake.
- **Mention any damage.** Broken frames, loose springs or reclining mechanisms help us plan how to carry the sofa safely.

## How much does sofa disposal cost in Dubai?

Every sofa removal is priced on the job in front of us. You'll know the price before any work starts, and these are the factors that change it:

- **Size and weight.** A two-seater is quicker to move than a seven-seat corner unit or a heavy leather recliner.
- **Dismantling.** Some sofas need legs, arms or modules removed to fit through doors and lifts.
- **Access.** Floor number, service lift availability, stairs and the distance from the door to the truck all matter.
- **How many items.** Adding armchairs, a mattress or other furniture to the same visit is usually better value than booking separately.
- **Timing.** Same-day collections depend on the day's schedule.

> **Tip:** Send one photo of the whole sofa and one showing the route out (hallway, lift, stairs). It's the fastest way to get an accurate price.

## Removing a sofa from an apartment tower vs a villa

Where the sofa lives changes how we plan the job.

### Apartments and towers

In areas like [Dubai Marina](/dubai-marina/) and [Downtown Dubai](/downtown-dubai/), most sofas leave through a **service lift and loading bay**. Before collection day:

- Ask building management whether the service lift needs booking and for which time slot.
- Tell security the removal company is coming; many towers ask for this in advance.
- Measure the sofa against the service lift door. If it's tight, we'll dismantle it on site.
- Check whether the building has set hours for moving furniture.

### Villas and townhouses

In villa communities and areas like [Jumeirah Village Circle](/jumeirah-village-circle/), access is usually easier, but there's often more to move: majlis seating, garden furniture and storeroom items. Let us know about side gates, steps and anything in the garden so we bring enough hands. Community rules sometimes limit removal hours, so check with your management if you're unsure.

## Can I use the free Dubai Municipality bulky waste service for a sofa?

Possibly. Dubai Municipality runs a [free bulky waste service](https://www.dm.gov.ae/dubai-municipality-promotes-waste-segregation-through-free-bulky-waste-disposal-service/) for used household furniture, appliances and electronics, requested via WhatsApp on 800900. However, residents of real estate development zones and free zones can't apply, which rules out many freehold communities. Collections are also scheduled after your request rather than on demand.

If you don't qualify, need the sofa carried out from inside the apartment, or need it gone before a deadline such as a tenancy handover or new delivery, a private collection is the quicker option.

## Why you shouldn't leave a sofa by the bins

It's tempting to drag an old sofa down to the bin area and hope it disappears. Don't. Dumping waste in public places is a fined offence under Dubai's waste regulations (see [Executive Council Resolution No. 14 of 2015](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2015/Executive%20Council%20Resolution%20No.%20(14)%20of%202015.html)). Buildings and communities may also pass on clean-up costs to residents. A proper collection avoids the risk and the hassle.
