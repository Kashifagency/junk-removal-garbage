An office move or lease handover leaves more than desks to deal with: chairs, cabinets, packaging, pantry appliances and equipment can all be left behind. **Office clearance in Dubai** works best when you decide what goes, secure your business data, and arrange access before the collection day. This guide gives office managers a workable checklist, explains the items our crew can take, and shows how to request pricing for a full or partial clear-out.

## How does office clearance in Dubai work?

**Send photos and an item list, confirm building access, agree the price, and arrange collection.** A clear scope keeps a handover from turning into a series of last-minute decisions. The same steps work for a single floor, a small office suite or a storage room attached to a workplace.

1. Walk through the premises and mark each item **keep**, **remove**, or **check with the landlord**. Include cupboards, meeting rooms, pantry and storage areas.
2. Send us photos of each room and a short list of heavy or awkward items. Tell us your location, floor, deadline and the nearest loading point.
3. Ask building management about lift reservations, security registration, loading bay hours and any move-out permission. Share those conditions before agreeing a collection time.
4. We confirm what the job includes and give clear pricing before work starts. Our crew then carries the agreed items out, loads them and takes them for responsible handling, with materials recycled whenever possible.

For a staged move, agree which zone will be cleared first. If staff are still working, mark the items to remove with a simple label and keep walkways open. A room-by-room list also makes the final check with your facilities or property manager easier.

## What should stay out of a general office clearance?

**Keep confidential material, hazardous items and anything that belongs to the landlord out of the removal pile until the right person has approved it.** This step protects your business and prevents a costly handover dispute.

Back up and erase data on laptops, servers, hard drives, copiers and phones before handing them over. Remove client files, financial records and employee information from cabinets and desks. A general junk removal visit does not provide certified data destruction or secure document shredding. If your policy requires certificates, use a specialist provider and ask them about their process directly.

Do not put batteries that are damaged or swollen, chemicals, paint, solvents or other hazardous materials into a normal clearance load. For fixed partitions, lighting, network wiring or built-in cabinetry, confirm the lease and building rules first. A licensed trade may need to disconnect or dismantle certain fittings before our collection can start.

> **Tip:** Photograph the office after staff have removed personal belongings but before the clearance begins. Keep that record alongside the approved removal list for the landlord handover.

## What affects the price of office clearance?

**The main factors are volume, item type, access and timing.** A handful of chairs near a loading bay is a different job from multiple floors of furniture reached through a booked service lift. We do not use one flat price for every office.

Tell us the number of rooms, rough quantities and any especially large pieces. Wide photos show how much space the load will take, while a close-up can reveal a difficult item such as a large boardroom table. Please say if furniture is already dismantled; do not assume dismantling is included for fixed or complex pieces.

Access affects the work too. A long walk from suite to service lift, a narrow corridor, stairs, a distant loading bay or a restricted collection window can change the crew and time needed. Share the building's instructions with your photos so the price reflects the real job. If the office must be empty by a specific date, say so early; same-day or next-day service is available subject to scheduling.

## How do building rules change the plan?

**In many Dubai commercial buildings, the building decides when and how bulky items may move.** Ask the facilities team about a service lift slot, loading bay reservation, contractor entry details and any protection required for floors or lift walls. Rules vary by property, so confirm the current requirements for your own building.

In a [Downtown Dubai office](/downtown-dubai/), the loading route may run through a shared basement and a booked goods lift. In an [Al Barsha business building](/al-barsha/), the issue may be parking or a short loading window. Neither location alone predicts the work; the actual door-to-vehicle route does. Send a quick photo or description of that route when arranging collection.

If the office is on multiple floors, plan where removed items can wait without blocking corridors or fire exits. Ask security whether the vehicle needs pre-registration. Keep a contact person available on site to decide about unexpected items and sign off when the agreed rooms are empty.

## How should you prepare for a lease handover?

**Separate the landlord's fixtures from your company's property before the crew arrives.** Start with the lease, fit-out approvals and handover checklist. Some items that look like ordinary office furniture may be part of the leased space; others may belong to a supplier under an equipment agreement.

Work backwards from the handover date. Decide when IT will collect devices, when staff will empty drawers, when movers will take retained furniture, and when our crew can remove the rest. If a cleaner is booked, schedule cleaning after the clearance: our service removes the agreed junk but does not replace a professional end-of-lease clean.

For a shop or showroom, make a separate inventory of display units and fixtures. For a workplace renovation, ask the fit-out contractor who will manage stripped materials and construction debris. These decisions stop two teams from arriving for the same pile, or each assuming the other will take it.

## Is municipal bulky-waste pickup suitable for offices?

**Do not plan an office clearance around the free household bulky-waste service.** Dubai Municipality describes that service as a free option for individuals disposing of used household furniture, appliances and electronics; it excludes real-estate development zones and free zones. Check the [Dubai Municipality bulky-waste guidance](https://www.dm.gov.ae/dubai-municipality-promotes-waste-segregation-through-free-bulky-waste-disposal-service/) and current eligibility if you have a separate household item to dispose of.

An office clear-out usually needs a crew to carry items from the premises, coordinate with building management, and work to a business handover date. Those are the details to include when you contact a private service.
