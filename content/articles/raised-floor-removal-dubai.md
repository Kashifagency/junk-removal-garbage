## Types of raised access flooring systems we clear

Commercial facilities in Dubai utilize a variety of elevated flooring configurations depending on cabling density and weight load requirements. We clear all major systems:

- **Steel-encapsulated woodcore panels:** High-density particleboard cores fully wrapped in galvanized sheet steel, commonly installed in general corporate offices and commercial bank branches.
- **Calcium sulphate access tiles:** Solid, non-combustible mineral composite panels offering high acoustic damping and fire resistance, widely used in premium DIFC executive suites.
- **Hollow steel cementitious panels:** Formed welded steel shells filled with lightweight structural concrete, standard in high-traffic commercial office towers and call centres.
- **Perforated airflow grilles and ventilation tiles:** Aluminum and heavy-gauge steel perforated panels used for precision HVAC airflow distribution in server rooms.
- **Finished surface access panels:** Panels pre-bonded with anti-static vinyl (ESD), conductive rubber, ceramic tiles, or commercial hard-wearing carpet.
- **Underfloor support hardware:** Galvanized steel pedestal jacks, height-adjusting threaded collars, gaskets, sound-dampening pedestal caps, and perimeter locking stringer bars.

## Tenancy handover standards and landlord reinstatement in Dubai

Commercial leasing in Dubai's premier business districts is governed by formal lease agreements requiring strict property reinstatement:

### Reinstating offices back to base-build "Cat A" condition

When corporate leases expire in high-profile developments such as ICD Brookfield Place, Emaar Square, or Emirates Financial Towers, landlords enforce rigorous handover inspections. If a tenant installed raised flooring over the base building's concrete slab to route power and IT connections, the lease agreement typically requires the floor to be dismantled and cleared before the security deposit is released. Our team ensures the concrete subfloor is completely cleared of pedestals, adhesives, and dust, leaving the space fully compliant with landlord handover standards.

### Removing structural epoxy and concrete anchors cleanly

Support pedestals in commercial offices are secured either with expansion anchor bolts drilled into the concrete or with structural polyurethane adhesives. Improper removal using sledgehammers can fracture the underlying screed, resulting in costly back-charges from building management. Our crews use pneumatic impact tools and angled shearing blades to remove pedestals cleanly at the base, followed by mechanical surface scraping to eliminate lingering adhesive residues.

## Protecting high-rise service elevators and weight thresholds

Managing the transit of heavy access flooring panels through multi-storey corporate towers requires careful logistical management:

- **Elevator payload capacity monitoring:** A stack of just 20 steel-encapsulated panels weighs over 250 kg. Overloading service elevators can trigger safety cut-outs and cause mechanical shutdowns. Disposing of commercial raised access flooring requires strict environmental adherence under local waste management regulations:

Under Dubai Municipality Law No. 18 of 2024 Regulating Waste Management and circulars for commercial demolitions, fit-out materials cannot be dumped indiscriminately. Commercial operators are required to segregate recyclable metals, wood, and mineral compounds directly on site. Our clearance team separates steel pedestals, aluminum airflow grilles, and composite panels into distinct categories before leaving the property.

### Material reclamation in Al Quoz recycling depots

The materials comprising raised flooring systems possess substantial recycling utility.
