## Step-by-step garden deck dismantling and ground restoration

Dismantling outdoor decking across Dubai villa plots demands systematic deconstruction to protect underground irrigation pipes, electrical lighting conduits, and boundary masonry:

- **Locating underground irrigation and electrical services:** Before digging or cutting into garden ground, our team identifies irrigation lines, drip pipes, and low-voltage landscape lighting cables buried beneath the deck. We carefully isolate these services to prevent costly pipe ruptures.
- **Surface board unfastening and clip extraction:** Where screw heads are accessible, our technicians use high-torque cordless impact drivers to unthread fasteners cleanly. For hidden clip systems, we unclip locking brackets sequentially, stacking boards neatly for compact loading.
- **Managing seized and rusted fasteners:** In coastal villa areas like Palm Jumeirah and Jumeirah, salt air and irrigation mist cause standard steel screws to shear or corrode. Our crew uses bi-metal reciprocating blades to cut joist fasteners horizontally beneath the plank without splintering timber or creating hazardous airborne sawdust.
- **Sub-frame breakdown and bearer dismantling:** Once the surface boards are cleared, we dismantle the structural grid of cross-joists and ledger boards. Heavy timber beams anchored to villa walls or perimeter boundary fences are unbolted with socket wrenches, ensuring exterior render and waterproofing remains intact.
- **Footing excavation and ground stud grinding:** Where support posts are set into poured concrete footings or attached to masonry patios with expansion anchors, our team excavates the footings or cuts steel anchor studs flush below the soil surface using high-speed angle grinders.
- **Ground raking, debris magnetic sweep, and sand levelling:** We finish every deck removal by raking the bare ground, pulling out weed barrier membranes, and running heavy-duty magnetic sweepers to capture dropped nails, staples, and screw fragments, leaving the yard safe for pets, children, and garden handovers.

## What factors determine decking removal pricing?

Because residential decks vary significantly in surface area, elevation, and anchoring methods, pricing is calculated based on clear project parameters:

- **Total square-metre footprint:** The overall area of the decking directly governs crew size, tool requirements, and transport truck capacity.
- **Decking material and structural density:** Heavy exotic hardwoods like Ipe and Teak weigh substantially more than hollow composite boards or modular pine duckboards, impacting vehicle payload limits.
- **Sub-frame elevation and concrete foundations:** Ground-level floating deck tiles lift off quickly, whereas elevated decks set on concrete footings, brick piers, or welded steel sub-frames require heavy mechanical demolition.
- **Villa access pathways and hauling distance:** Broad side gates with direct driveway access facilitate rapid removal, while narrow side walkways requiring manual hauling around swimming pools or through interior corridors require additional labour.

> **Tip:** Clear outdoor furniture, barbecue grills, and potted plants off the deck before our crew arrives. This allows our technicians to start unfastening boards immediately upon arrival.
