6. **Controlled winch lowering and disposal:** The lightened structural framework is secured with heavy-duty lifting slings, unbolted from the ceiling toggle bolts or unhooked from the motorized ceiling winch, and lowered smoothly to floor level. Ceiling conductors are capped with insulated twist connectors, the site is thoroughly vacuumed for glass grit, and all materials are loaded for recycling.
