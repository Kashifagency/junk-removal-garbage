## Regulatory compliance and municipal waste management rules

Commercial waste disposal in Dubai is strictly governed by municipal environmental directives. Under [Law No. 18 of 2024 Regulating Waste Management](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2024/Law%20No.%20(18)%20of%202024%20Regulating%20Waste%20Management.html) and [Executive Council Resolution No. 14 of 2015](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2015/Executive%20Council%20Resolution%20No.%20(14)%20of%202015.html), dumping commercial kitchen waste or construction debris in unauthorised areas carries fines ranging from AED 5,000 to AED 50,000.

Furthermore, commercial refrigeration units contain regulated refrigerants (such as R404A, R134a, or R410A) that must never be vented into the atmosphere. Our partner recovery facilities ensure refrigerants are recovered lawfully by certified HVAC technicians before scrap metal shredding. Under Dubai Municipality Circular 12-10-1 (2023), metals, electrical motors, and insulated panels must be segregated at source, ensuring maximum resource recovery.
