## Practical steps to prepare sanitary fixtures before our team arrives

Taking a few quick preparatory steps ensures your bathroom fixture clearance proceeds smoothly, quickly, and cleanly on collection day:

1. **Shut off isolation valves completely:** Turn off the water angle valves located behind the toilet cistern or underneath the washbasin counter. Ensure the supply is fully stopped before attempting to unbolt pipework.
2. **Flush the cistern and sponge out residual water:** Depress the flush handle to empty the cistern tank completely. Use a sponge or towel to soak up the remaining standing water at the bottom of the toilet bowl and trapway to avoid accidental spills during lifting.
3. **Unbolt floor anchors and wall mounting brackets:** Loosen the floor mounting bolts securing the toilet base to the floor flange, or back out the wall studs supporting a wall-hung WC or wall-mounted basin.
4. **Tape down loose lids and cover cracked edges:** If an old toilet bowl or basin has a pre-existing hairline fracture or sharp broken chip, apply heavy-duty duct tape over the affected area. This reinforces structural stability during transport and protects our team's hands from jagged ceramic edges.
5. **Clear a direct corridor pathway:** Remove bath mats, laundry baskets, and personal toiletries from the bathroom floor and adjacent hallways to provide our crew with an unobstructed exit route to the service elevator.

## Why Dubai Municipality bulky waste excludes toilets and sinks

Many Dubai residents assume that the municipal free bulky waste collection service (accessible via WhatsApp at 800900) will pick up old toilets and bathroom sinks. However, official Dubai Municipality guidelines strictly exclude all bathroom sanitary ware and plumbing fixtures.

The municipal collection service is designed solely for domestic furniture (such as sofas and beds) and basic household electrical appliances. Disconnected toilets, basins, and bidets are classified by the Waste Management Department as renovation, demolition, and home maintenance waste. Consequently, municipal collection crews will reject sanitary fixtures left for collection. 

Leaving discarded toilets or basins on public pavements, beside community waste bins, or in communal apartment corridors violates municipal cleanliness laws. Under [Executive Council Resolution No. 14 of 2015](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2015/Executive%20Council%20Resolution%20No.%20(14)%20of%202015.html) and [Law No. 18 of 2024](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2024/Law%20No.%20(18)%20of%202024%20Regulating%20Waste%20Management.html) Regulating Waste Management in the Emirate of Dubai, illegal dumping of renovation and ceramic waste attracts substantial municipal fines ranging from AED 500 up to AED 10,000.
