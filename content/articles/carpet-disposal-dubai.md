## Tenancy handover requirements in Dubai (EJARI & security deposits)

In the UAE, residential tenancy contracts governed by the Dubai Land Department (DLD) and EJARI require tenants to return properties in their original condition at handover:

### Removing tenant-installed carpets
Expatriate tenants frequently install wall-to-wall carpeting in bedrooms to create a cozy atmosphere over cold ceramic tiles. However, upon move-out, landlords and real estate leasing agents routinely demand that all non-original floor coverings be removed. Failure to remove carpets can result in landlords deducting substantial contractor fees directly from your security deposit.

### Tack strip removal and floor damage prevention
Fitted carpets are held taut along room perimeters by wooden gripper strips anchored into concrete floors with steel masonry nails. Prying up these strips requires finesse; pulling them carelessly can gouge concrete or shatter adjoining skirting tiles. Our crew uses precision flat pry bars to remove tack strips cleanly.

### Office reinstatement (Make-Good clauses)
Commercial lease contracts in business hubs like [Al Barsha](/al-barsha/) and Downtown Dubai include rigorous "make-good" reinstatement clauses. Commercial tenants must strip all branded carpet tiles, reception flooring, and adhesive underlays back to bare screed before the commercial landlord issues final clearance certificates.

## What affects the price of carpet disposal in Dubai?

We believe in complete pricing transparency with no hidden add-ons. The cost of carpet removal is calculated based on simple practical criteria:

### Square meterage and volume
A compact 12-square-meter bedroom carpet requires minimal truck volume, whereas a 300-square-meter corporate office floor requires multiple truckloads and substantial labor.

### Uplift vs pre-rolled pickup
If you or your fit-out contractors have already stripped, rolled, and tied the carpets into bundles ready at the door, collection is faster and more cost-effective. If our crew performs complete uplift, underlay detachment, and nail-strip removal, labor is factored into the upfront price.

### Type of installation (glued vs stretched)
Carpets stretched over perimeter gripper rods unhook relatively quickly. Commercial carpet tiles or direct-glued broadloom carpets bonded with strong tackifier adhesives require extensive mechanical scraping to detach from concrete subfloors.

### Building access and elevator transport
Carrying dense 3-meter carpet rolls down staircases or maneuvering them into tight residential elevators requires careful crew coordination. Buildings with direct basement loading docks and high-ceiling freight elevators allow more rapid transport.

## High-rise building access and logistics in Dubai

Transporting long carpet rolls through Dubai residential towers requires careful planning:

### Freight elevator clearance
Standard residential passenger elevators often measure under 2.1 meters in cab height, making it impossible to stand long 3-meter or 4-meter carpet rolls upright. Our crew cuts carpets on site into manageable 1.5-meter to 2-meter widths before rolling and binding, ensuring effortless passage into standard elevators without bending or jamming.

### Move-out permits and security clearances
Towers managed by developers like Emaar, Nakheel, and DAMAC require contractors to register before carrying large bulky materials through common areas. We coordinate with building security desks and adhere to designated service elevator hours (typically 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM).

### Dust containment
Pulling up aged carpets often releases years of accumulated trapped sand and fiber dust. Our team uses drop cloths and sweeps subfloors clean immediately following removal to maintain fresh air quality and avoid tracking fine dust into building corridors.
