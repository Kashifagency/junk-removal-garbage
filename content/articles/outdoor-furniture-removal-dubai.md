## Why Dubai weather degrades exterior furniture so quickly

Even high-end outdoor furniture manufactured for international markets struggles to withstand the demanding UAE climate:

### Extreme ultraviolet (UV) radiation
Dubai experiences over 300 days of intense, unfiltered sunlight annually. High ultraviolet index levels degrade polymer bonds in synthetic polyethylene rattan, causing it to become dry, brittle, and snap under normal weight. Synthetic fabric cushions also lose their waterproofing and bleach dramatically.

### Blistering summer temperatures
During the peak summer months from June to September, ambient temperatures regularly exceed 45°C, with dark patio surfaces reaching over 60°C. This extreme thermal cycling causes wood to shrink and expand rapidly, resulting in deep warping, hairline fractures, and loosened joinery.

### Coastal humidity and salt-spray corrosion
In coastal areas such as Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Marina, and JBR, humid sea air contains fine salt particles that rapidly penetrate micro-cracks in protective powder coatings. Within a year, exposed steel hinges, bolts, and frames develop bubbling rust that compromises structural safety.

### Fine desert sand and dust accumulation
Frequent seasonal shamal winds carry fine desert dust into the deep recesses of wicker weave and lounger joints. Combined with rain showers or balcony irrigation water, this creates stubborn grime that stains fabric cushions and accelerates material deterioration.
