## Mall handover and landlord de-fit requirements in Dubai

Vacating a restaurant space in a major Dubai development involves strict contractual and administrative requirements. Whether located in a shopping mall, an urban promenade, or a corporate hotel tower, commercial tenants face rigorous handover standards:

### Core and shell lease handovers
Commercial tenancy agreements in Dubai frequently require tenants to return units to "core and shell" or "base build" condition. This means removing all non-structural tenant installations, including floor tiles, bar partitions, kitchen equipment, and decorative wall cladding. Failing to complete the clearance within the agreed handover window can result in landlords withholding significant security deposits or charging daily overstay penalties.

### Mall loading dock permits and night-shift clearance
Major retail destinations such as Dubai Mall, Mall of the Emirates, and [Dubai Marina](/dubai-marina/) Mall do not permit commercial furniture or waste removal during daytime retail hours. All heavy clearance work must occur during the night shift, typically between 11:00 PM and 7:00 AM. Our operations team coordinates with mall management to secure contractor gate passes, register delivery vehicles, and complete silent, clean removal without disrupting adjoining businesses.

### Protecting common areas and service elevators
Dubai commercial property managers closely inspect shared building assets before issuing clearance certificates. Dragging heavy commercial ovens across tiled corridors or damaging service lift doors can lead to instant building management fines. Our team uses protective floor coverings, neoprene corner guards, and heavy pneumatic-wheel dollies to ensure common areas remain spotless and undamaged.
