## Structural machinery dismantling and anchor bolt extraction

One of the most demanding aspects of a factory cleanout in Dubai is removing machinery bolted directly into reinforced industrial concrete slabs. Landlord tenancy handover guidelines stipulate that floor slabs must be delivered free of protruding anchor studs, jagged bolts, or structural floor hazards.

Our technicians utilise industrial reciprocating saws, rotary hammer drills, and heavy-duty angle grinders to safely detach machines from their foundation pads. Once the equipment is lifted onto heavy-load skates and extracted, our crew grinds all protruding steel chemical anchors, sleeve bolts, and wedge studs flush with the concrete surface. This level of preparation ensures your facility manager or landlord signs off the slab condition without demanding costly floor resurfacing penalties.
