## Why you should never dump potting soil in municipal garbage chutes

When faced with moving out, some residents attempt to discard potting soil and dead plants down building garbage chutes or inside communal bin rooms. This practice violates municipal health standards and building management codes for several reasons:

- **Chute blockages and compactor damage:** Dense, damp potting soil dropped down multi-storey chutes packs into solid blockages that jam compaction mechanisms and take chutes out of service for the entire building.
- **Dust sensor alarms:** Pouring dry potting mix into chutes creates fine airborne dust clouds that frequently trigger optical fire alarms in tower bin rooms, resulting in emergency building evacuations and security penalties.
- **Odour and drainage contamination:** Wet organic matter rotting inside unventilated waste rooms attracts pests and releases foul odours, leading to immediate tenant complaints and management fines.

Our team ensures all soil and organic materials are collected in sealed, tear-proof sacks and transported directly to designated municipal organic processing facilities.
