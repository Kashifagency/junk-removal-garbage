## What influences greenhouse removal pricing in Dubai?

We believe in upfront, transparent pricing without hidden surcharges or sudden on-site price jumps. Several key physical factors influence the service cost for greenhouse removal:

- **Structure dimensions and footprint:** A compact 2m x 2m hobby greenhouse requires modest disassembly time and half a truckload, whereas an extensive 6m x 4m commercial-style garden enclosure demands full-day multi-person dismantling.
- **Glazing type and condition:** Polycarbonate panels are lightweight and shatterproof, allowing swift extraction. Aged, cracked glass panes require careful taping, suction handling, and padded transport to ensure zero glass fragments enter the lawn.
- **Ground anchoring method:** Greenhouses secured with simple ground pegs or bolted into paving slabs can be unfastened quickly. Structures anchored into solid poured concrete footing blocks require excavation or mechanical grinding.
- **Villa garden access distance:** Clearances from villas with wide side-access gates allowing direct trolley transit to our truck require less labour than narrow pathways requiring manual carrying around swimming pools and patio furniture.

## Community handover regulations and municipal waste directives

Rented villas in Dubai are subject to clear restoration covenants under tenancy agreements regulated by the Real Estate Regulatory Agency (RERA). Landlords and master developer inspectors require garden additions—such as temporary greenhouses, pergolas, and storage sheds—to be removed unless the incoming tenant formally signs an architectural waiver. Failure to clear garden structures before the final lease inspection frequently results in heavy deductions from the tenant's security deposit.

Furthermore, under [Law No. 18 of 2024 Regulating Waste Management](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2024/Law%20No.%20(18)%20of%202024%20Regulating%20Waste%20Management.html) and [Executive Council Resolution No. 14 of 2015](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2015/Executive%20Council%20Resolution%20No.%20(14)%20of%202015.html), dumping dismantled aluminium, glass, or plastic waste on open desert plots or outside communal green waste bins incurs fines ranging from AED 5,000 to AED 50,000. Our licensed service guarantees lawful, documented transport to authorized recycling facilities.

## Sustainable materials recycling and eco-friendly recovery

Garden structures consist of valuable, highly recyclable raw materials that should never end up in municipal landfills:

- **Extruded aluminium framing:** Aluminium structural posts, glazing bars, and base rails are segregated and delivered to licensed metallurgical smelters in Al Quoz, where aluminium is melted down with 95% energy savings compared to primary extraction.
- **Polycarbonate and acrylic sheets:** Clean polycarbonate roof and wall panels are sorted, bundled, and routed to licensed plastics recovery plants to be shredded and granulated into secondary polymer feedstock.
- **Tempered and float glass:** Intact and broken glass panels are transported to dedicated mineral recycling crushers for use in industrial aggregate and reflective road markings.
- **Galvanised steel hardware and fittings:** Corner brackets, anchor bolts, and bracing cables are reclaimed for industrial ferrous scrap smelting in Jebel Ali.
