## Step-by-step: how our crew dismantles and removes rooftop tanks

Dismantling a heavy water tank perched two or three storeys above ground requires methodical execution, proper equipment, and uncompromising safety standards:

1. **Water isolation and residual pump-out:** Before dismantling begins, our technicians verify that the main municipal water feed is isolated and that electrical power to booster pumps is shut off at the breaker. If residual water remains trapped below the drain outlet, we deploy portable submersible transfer pumps and heavy discharge hoses to drain the bottom volume directly into external downspouts.
2. **Roof membrane and parapet padding:** To protect your roof waterproof coating from falling tools or sharp composite edges, we lay thick rubber mats and protective canvas tarpaulins across the plinth and surrounding walkways. We also place heavy foam corner protectors over decorative parapet mouldings and coping stones where lowering ropes will pass.
3. **Spark-free reciprocating sectional cutting:** Using heavy-duty electric reciprocating saws fitted with specialised composite-cutting blades, our technicians cut the tank into manageable curved panels measuring roughly 1 metre by 1.5 metres. We avoid rotary grinders that generate sparks or hot dust, ensuring clean, safe cuts without scorching roof membranes or creating airborne hazards for neighbouring properties.
4. **Controlled rope descent and facade rigging:** Each cut panel is individually secured with heavy nylon rigging ropes. A team member stationed on the roof guides the panel out over the parapet edge while ground crew members control the descent line, landing each piece smoothly on padded ground tarpaulins away from landscaping, parked vehicles, or glass windows.
5. **Ground staging, vehicle loading, and site sweep:** Once all tank sections, pipe fittings, and booster pumps reach ground level, our crew carries the materials directly to our waiting collection truck. We finish the job by sweeping the rooftop concrete plinth, vacuuming stray plastic shavings, and inspecting the area to leave your roof clean and ready for your plumber.

## What factors affect water tank removal pricing?

We provide completely transparent, upfront pricing with zero hidden charges. Several practical factors influence the final cost of water tank removal:

## Eco-friendly recycling: where does dismantled water tank waste go?

Managing bulky synthetic waste responsibly is central to Dubai’s environmental sustainability objectives under the UAE Circular Economy Policy. Water tanks are made from durable industrial materials that do not naturally biodegrade and must never end up in municipal landfills. When our crew clears a dismantled water tank from your property, every component is routed to certified material recovery facilities in Al Quoz and Dubai Industrial City:

- **Polyethylene granulation and remanufacturing:** Rotomoulded HDPE tank panels are transported to dedicated polymer reclamation facilities where they are washed, shredded into flakes, and melted into secondary plastic resin pellets. These pellets are repurposed by UAE manufacturers to produce non-potable agricultural irrigation pipes, heavy-duty drainage conduit, and industrial storage bins.
- **Glass-Reinforced Plastic (GRP) downcycling:** Fiberglass composite panels are processed through industrial crushers and hammer mills. The pulverised fiberglass aggregate is incorporated as high-strength reinforcing filler in precast concrete products, road asphalt sub-bases, and architectural paving stones.
- **Scrap metal reclamation:** Brass isolation valves, copper manifold joints, and galvanised steel mounting struts are separated on site. These non-ferrous and ferrous metals are melted down in licensed metal refineries to manufacture new industrial hardware, saving valuable energy compared to raw ore extraction.
- **Pressure pumps and electrical components:** Redundant booster pumps contain high-value copper motor windings, aluminium cooling fins, and steel shafts. Certified recyclers dismantle the pump casings, recover the copper coils for electric motor remanufacturing, and ensure zero electronic waste enters the environment.

## Why private removal beats municipal waste collection

Dubai Municipality operates an excellent free bulk waste collection service via WhatsApp 800900, but municipal regulations strictly bar building fixtures, plumbing systems, sanitaryware, and construction debris. Municipal collection workers operate exclusively at street level from roadside curbs. They do not carry tools, are prohibited from entering private properties, will not ascend to rooftops, and will not dismantle large structures.

Abandoning an old water tank on a community sidewalk or beside public trash bins violates Dubai waste disposal regulations and risks municipal fines of up to AED 1,000 under Local Resolution No. 14 of 2015.
