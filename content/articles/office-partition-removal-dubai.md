## Commercial tower compliance: NOC approvals, loading bays, and night de-fit curfews

Conducting interior deconstruction in Dubai's premier business towers demands rigorous coordination with commercial building management and facilities administration.

### Building NOC documentation and work permits

Premier office towers across Dubai—such as those managed by Dubai Multi Commodities Centre (DMCC), DIFC Authority, Dubai Properties, and Emaar Commercial—require detailed contractor submittals prior to issuing an interior work permit. Requirements include valid commercial trade licences, comprehensive third-party liability insurance coverage, risk assessment method statements (RAMS), and technician credentials. Our operations team prepares and submits these documentation packages directly, eliminating administrative delays and ensuring smooth permit issuance.

### Night shift de-fit curfews and freight elevator logistics

In corporate business towers, any work generating vibration, drilling noise, or dust is strictly barred during normal office operating hours (8:00 AM to 6:00 PM). Building management mandates that demolition and partition dismantling take place during evening de-fit windows—typically from 8:00 PM to 6:00 AM—or across weekends. Our crew is specifically rostered for overnight commercial shifts, reserving tower loading bays and freight lifts in advance so transport trucks are loaded without holding up morning office operations.

### Protecting raised access floors, carpet tiles, and corridor finishes

Most Dubai commercial towers feature raised access flooring systems containing delicate power and data distribution cabling beneath carpet tiles. Carelessly dropping heavy glass panels or dragging steel studs can crack calcium sulphate floor panels or tear commercial carpet tiles, generating costly repair back-charges from building landlords. Our crew installs heavy corrugated floor protection sheets, uses non-marking pneumatic rubber wheels on all handcarts, and lines common elevator vestibules with protective wall pads throughout the clearance.
