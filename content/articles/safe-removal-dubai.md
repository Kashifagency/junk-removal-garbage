## Dubai building logistics: floor loads, lift limits, and security clearances

Navigating heavy safes out of high-rise commercial towers and gated villa communities across Dubai requires meticulous planning and strict adherence to facility rules.

### Floor load distribution and luxury surface protection

Residential and commercial buildings in central Dubai boast expensive flooring materials such as polished Crema Marfil marble, imported travertine, and engineered oak parquet. When a 300 kg safe rests on four tiny metal feet, the pressure exerted on floor tiles exceeds hundreds of pounds per square inch. Dragging or rolling unprotected steel wheels across these surfaces instantly leaves permanent gouges or cracks tile edges. Our crew lays interlocking composite distribution runners that disperse point pressure across broad surface areas, allowing the safe to glide across fragile floors safely.

In high-rise developments across [Downtown Dubai](/downtown-dubai/) and [DIFC](/junk-removal-difc/), moving heavy machinery requires building management approval and booking the designated service elevator. When loading a heavy safe alongside crew members and dollies, elevator weight capacities must be verified in advance.
