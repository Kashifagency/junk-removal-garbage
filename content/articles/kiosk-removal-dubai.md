## Step-by-step kiosk deconstruction in shopping malls

De-fitting a commercial kiosk inside a functioning Dubai retail galleria requires disciplined engineering steps to prevent structural accidents and safeguard surrounding retail tenants:

- **Site perimeter protection and floor isolation:** Before unthreading a single bolt, our team covers the surrounding concourse floor with heavy canvas tarpaulins and interlocking shock-absorbing rubber mats. This prevents dropped tools or sliding panels from chipping costly granite, marble, or porcelain floor finishes.
- **Electrical isolation and lighting de-wiring:** Once the mall facilities team or retail manager confirms the main underfloor circuit breaker is switched off, our crew disconnects low-voltage transformers, LED strip drivers, and internal power distribution blocks. All wires are neatly coiled and prepared for electronics recycling.
- **Tempered glass showcase de-glazing:** Glass panels pose severe shatter hazards if flexed or torqued. We deploy industrial vacuum suction lifters to gently unseat glass showcases, wrapping each pane in corrugated cardboard and heavy-gauge bubble wrap before loading them onto padded A-frame glass dollies.
- **Cabinetry sectioning and modular breakdown:** Counters and display cabinetry are disassembled along factory modular joints. Where units are glued or blind-screwed, our technicians use precision oscillating tools to break joinery seams cleanly without creating excessive airborne dust.
- **Chassis unbolting and anchor remediation:** Metal sub-frames bolted to baseplates are unbolted with pneumatic impact wrenches. Any concrete or sub-floor anchor bolts are unthreaded cleanly or ground flush in accordance with private landlord base-build demising guidelines.
- **Concourse cleaning and tape residue removal:** We scrape away double-sided structural foam tapes, silicone sealants, and floor cable duct adhesive using citrus-based non-abrasive solvents, leaving the concourse surface pristine.

## What factors affect kiosk removal pricing?

Because retail kiosks differ substantially in scale, engineering, and location, pricing is determined by specific project variables rather than a generic flat fee:

- **Kiosk dimensions and layout footprint:** A compact 2x2-metre standalone accessory pod requires less manpower and transit time than a sprawling 6x4-metre modular island featuring perimeter cashier desks and overhead canopies.
- **Construction materials and dismantling complexity:** Kiosks assembled with heavy structural steel, welded box tubing, and thick tempered glass require more intensive unbolting and cutting compared to modular timber and laminate assemblies.
- **Night-shift logistics and loading dock access:** Operating during designated overnight curfews requires dedicated night teams. Long transit distances between the retail atrium and basement service freight lifts also influence labour requirements.
- **Volume of recyclable and non-recyclable materials:** Heavy metals, wiring, and clean glass are sorted for material reclamation at licensed recycling centres in Al Quoz, while non-recyclable composite boards are disposed of at approved municipal transfer stations.

> **Tip:** Before collection day, ensure the mall engineering department has fully de-energised the underfloor power supply to your kiosk and isolated any plumbing connections for food-and-beverage stands. This eliminates work stoppages on the night of dismantling.
