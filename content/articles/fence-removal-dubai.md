## Meeting developer tenancy handover rules and securing move-out permits

In master-planned communities across Dubai, major property developers such as Emaar Properties, Nakheel, Dubai Properties, and Damac maintain strict community aesthetic covenants. When moving out of a rental villa, tenants are legally required to return the property to its original handover condition as documented in the initial tenancy agreement and Ejari inspection report.

Unapproved exterior alterations are the single most common reason for withheld Move-Out Permits in Dubai villa communities:

### Architectural compliance violations

Tenants frequently install bamboo screens or high wooden slat fences to gain privacy from adjoining villas or public walkways. However, if these additions were installed without an official developer No Objection Certificate (NOC) and municipal approval, community inspectors will flag them during the pre-handover property inspection. Developers consider weathered, unpainted, or leaning fences an aesthetic violation that devalues the community.

### Concrete damage and protruding anchor studs

Unskilled removal attempts frequently leave broken concrete curbs, cracked boundary walls, or dangerous, protruding metal anchor studs sticking out of the patio floor. If inspection officers identify jagged bolts or damaged perimeter stonework, the developer will retain a substantial portion of your security deposit or assign their own contractors at steep rates to repair the masonry.

Our fence removal specialists ensure full compliance with developer handover checklists. We carefully remove anchors, grind posts flush with the ground, and restore perimeter boundaries smoothly, allowing you to pass property inspections and secure your deposit refund without delay.
