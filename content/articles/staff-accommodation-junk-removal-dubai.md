## Regulatory compliance: MOHRE and municipal accommodation standards

Staff housing in the UAE is subject to strict regulatory oversight designed to safeguard worker welfare and facility hygiene. Ministerial directives and Dubai Municipality guidelines establish clear requirements for residential labour camps:

- **Bed spacing and ventilation:** Regulations prohibit excessive bed density and enforce minimum floor space per resident. Outdated triple-tier bunks or damaged bed frames must be decommissioned and removed to maintain legal ceiling clearances and airflow.
- **Hygiene and pest control protocols:** Worn, damaged foam mattresses easily harbor dust mites and bedbugs. Routine municipal health inspections mandate the replacement of soiled sleep surfaces to maintain hygienic dormitories.
- **Fire safety and corridor clearance:** Dubai Civil Defence strictly forbids storing surplus beds, broken lockers, or broken appliances in stairwells, corridors, or building exit routes. Emergency evacuation paths must remain completely unobstructed at all times.
- **Tenancy lease handovers:** When corporate master leases expire on multi-storey residential blocks, commercial landlords require units to be returned completely vacant, swept clean, and restored to baseline condition before releasing security deposits.

Our clearance team ensures your facility satisfies all inspection requirements, eliminating clutter and potential safety citations quickly and safely.
