New mattress on the way, old one leaning against the wall? **Mattress disposal in Dubai** is one of the most common jobs we do. It's also one of the most awkward for residents to handle alone. Mattresses are heavy, floppy and hard to grip, they rarely fit neatly in a passenger lift, and leaving one by the bins can cause problems with your building. This guide covers how bed and mattress removal works, what we take, and how to get it done quickly.

## How does mattress disposal work in Dubai?

Bed and mattress removal with us is straightforward:

1. **Send a photo** of the mattress and bed frame on WhatsApp, with your area and floor number.
2. **Know the price before we start**, based on how many items there are, their size and the access.
3. **We do the lifting.** The crew dismantles the bed frame if needed, carries everything out from inside your home and loads it onto our truck.
4. **Responsible disposal.** Metal springs, frames and wood are recycled whenever possible.

You don't need to drag anything to the lift or the loading bay. We collect from the bedroom.

We remove every common size and type:

## Why old mattresses are hard to get rid of

Mattresses are one of the bulkiest items in any home, and they bring a few specific challenges:

- **Size and shape.** A king mattress doesn't bend neatly into a lift, and it's hard for one person to carry safely.
- **Weight.** Spring and hybrid mattresses are heavier than they look.
- **Hygiene.** Old mattresses collect dust and stains, so most residents don't want them dragged through shared corridors uncovered.
- **Building rules.** Many towers don't allow large items to be left in bin rooms or common areas, even for a short time.

> **Tip:** Strip all bedding the day before and, if you have one, slip the mattress into a mattress bag or plastic cover. It keeps corridors and lifts clean on the way out.

## Signs it's time to replace your mattress

Not sure whether the old mattress really needs to go? These are the usual signs:

- **Visible sagging** or a dip where you sleep
- **Lumps, broken or poking springs**, or foam that no longer springs back
- **Noisy springs** every time you move
- **Stains, tears or a worn-out cover** that can't be cleaned
- **You sleep better elsewhere**, for example in a hotel or the guest room

If any of these sound familiar and a new mattress is on the way, book the old one's removal for the same day as the delivery. That way it doesn't sit in the corridor or living room.

## How to prepare for bed and mattress removal

- **Strip all bedding**, including mattress protectors and toppers you want to keep.
- **Empty storage divans and under-bed drawers** completely.
- **Clear the route** from the bedroom to the front door, moving rugs and fragile items out of the way.
- **Separate anything staying**, such as a headboard or bedside tables you're keeping.
- **Tell us about bunk beds or heavy frames** so we bring the right tools and crew.

## Dismantling bed frames before removal

Most bed frames don't fit through doorways in one piece, and big storage or king-size frames almost never do. Our crew brings the tools to dismantle wooden and metal frames, slatted bases, headboards and bunk beds on site. That protects your walls and door frames and makes loading quicker.

![Crew member dismantling an old wooden bed frame in a Dubai villa bedroom before removal](/images/blog/bed-and-mattress-removal-dubai-2.webp)

If you've already taken the frame apart, keep the parts together and let us know. It saves time on the day.

## How much does mattress disposal cost?

The price depends on the job, and you'll always know it before we start. The main factors are:

- **Number of items.** One mattress, or a mattress plus frame, bunk bed and wardrobe.
- **Size.** Single and double mattresses are quicker to move than king-size mattresses and storage divans.
- **Dismantling.** Frames that need taking apart add a little time.
- **Access.** Floor number, lift availability and the distance to the truck.
- **Timing.** Same-day collections depend on the day's schedule.

## Apartments, villas and hotels

### Apartments

In apartment buildings in areas like [Al Barsha](/al-barsha/) and [Jumeirah Village Circle](/jumeirah-village-circle/), mattresses usually leave through the service lift. Check with building management whether the lift needs booking and if there are set hours for moving furniture. Tell us your floor so we can plan the crew.

### Villas and townhouses

Villa jobs often include several bedrooms, maids' rooms and storerooms at once. We clear multiple mattresses and frames in a single visit, so you don't have to book separate collections.

### Hotels, serviced apartments and landlords

Hotels, guest houses and serviced apartments replace mattresses regularly, and landlords often refresh furnished units between tenants. We handle **bulk mattress removal** with flexible scheduling, working around check-ins, guests and building access. That's part of our wider [commercial waste management service](/services/commercial-waste-management/).

### Moving out of a furnished apartment

If you're leaving a furnished rental, check your inventory before booking removal. Mattresses and bed frames supplied by the landlord usually need to stay, or be replaced with the landlord's agreement. Only dispose of items you own, and photograph the bedrooms after removal so the handover condition is clear.

## Can Dubai Municipality collect my mattress for free?

Dubai Municipality offers a [free bulky waste service](https://www.dm.gov.ae/dubai-municipality-promotes-waste-segregation-through-free-bulky-waste-disposal-service/) for used household furniture, requested via WhatsApp on 800900. Residents of real estate development zones and free zones can't apply, though, and collections are scheduled after your request. If you're not eligible, need the mattress carried out from inside your home, or need it gone before a new delivery or a tenancy handover, a private collection is the faster route.

Whatever you choose, don't leave a mattress on the street or beside the bins. Dumping waste in public places is a fined offence under Dubai's waste regulations.
