## Dubai Municipality waste regulations and circulars

Disposing of construction and demolition rubble in the Emirate of Dubai is governed by comprehensive environmental legislation designed to eliminate illegal dumping and foster sustainable recycling:

### On-site sorting under Circular 12-10-1

Under Dubai Municipality Circular 12-10-1 (2023), engineering consultants, contractors, and waste generators are legally required to segregate building waste at source. Concrete and masonry must be separated from timber, metals, and packaging materials. Mixing masonry rubble with domestic waste or chemical solvents is strictly prohibited.

Our clearance operations follow these circular directives precisely. We maintain segregated loads so that concrete, blocks, and clean masonry can be transported straight to specialized concrete crushing plants where old masonry is pulverized into graded road base aggregates and sub-base fill for infrastructure projects.

### Severe penalties for illegal dumping

Under Dubai Municipality Executive Council Resolution No. 14 of 2015 and Law No. 18 of 2024 Regulating Waste Management, dumping construction debris in vacant desert plots, open suburban land, or public rights-of-way is a serious environmental offense. Municipal penalties for illegal fly-tipping range from AED 10,000 to AED 100,000, with vehicles subject to impoundment and repeat violations carrying double penalties.

Hiring unlicensed casual haulers advertising on street lamp posts or online classifieds carries substantial legal risk, as property owners can be held liable if waste traced back to their renovation is found dumped illegally. Our fully licensed commercial transport fleet guarantees compliant, verifiable disposal at approved Dubai Municipality recovery facilities.
