## Dubai Municipality regulations and concrete recycling

Managing masonry and demolition debris in Dubai is subject to strict environmental oversight under municipal law:

### Compliance with Circular 12-10-1 and Law No. 18 of 2024

Under Dubai Municipality Circular 12-10-1 regarding construction and demolition waste segregation, concrete masonry, paving stones, and screed sand cannot be mixed with municipal domestic waste. Discarding heavy paving stones in residential green waste containers or communal dumpsters breaches Law No. 18 of 2024 on public cleanliness, resulting in heavy municipal fines. Our operations comply fully with all municipal waste regulations, ensuring your property remains completely protected from regulatory violations.

### Concrete crushing and aggregate recovery in Al Quoz

Rather than taking up valuable space in local landfill sites, all collected interlock pavers and concrete curbs are routed directly to certified material recycling facilities in Al Quoz and Dubai Industrial City. Massive industrial crushers pulverize the old concrete into graded recycled aggregates (road base, sub-base, and pipe bedding), which are repurposed across regional infrastructure and civil construction projects, conserving natural quarry resources.
