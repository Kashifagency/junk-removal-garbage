## Material segregation and Dubai green recycling standards

Commercial construction and retail de-fits in Dubai are governed by strict environmental standards designed to maximize resource recovery:

### Dubai Municipality Circular 12-10-1 compliance

Under Dubai Municipality Circular 12-10-1, commercial contractors and waste haulers are legally obligated to separate demolition and fit-out waste on site. Discarding mixed construction debris into general municipal compactors is strictly prohibited and carries severe regulatory fines. Our teams systematically segregate materials during dismantling: clean timber framing is bundled separately from drywall panels, while metal studs and fixings are kept in dedicated steel scrap bins.

### Timber, drywall, and metal recovery pathways

All salvageable framing lumber and plywood sheets are routed to licensed timber recycling centres in Al Quoz and Jebel Ali, where untreated wood is shredded for composite board manufacturing, landscape mulching, or industrial biomass. Clean gypsum plasterboard core material is transferred to certified processing facilities to produce soil conditioning amendments and recycled drywall compounds, while scrap galvanized steel and aluminum extrusions are melted down for metal reuse.
