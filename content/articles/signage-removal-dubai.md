## Step-by-step dismantling across commercial facades

Commercial buildings in Dubai feature varied exterior cladding materials, each presenting distinct physical deconstruction challenges:

- **Aluminium composite panels (ACM/Alucobond):** Widespread across retail plazas and commercial towers. Signage contractors secure signage into ACM panels using rivets or self-drilling screws into steel substructures. Our technicians drill out rivets cleanly or unthread fasteners without using brute pry bars that crease aluminium skins. Hardened silicone beads are sliced with flexible polymer blades to protect factory fluoropolymer finishes.
- **Structural glass curtain walls & shopfronts:** High-end towers across DIFC and Downtown feature floor-to-ceiling glass storefronts. Signage mounted on glass uses structural VHB tapes or stainless standoff fixings. Our specialists apply controlled heat to soften adhesive bonds before releasing brackets with nylon filaments, unthreading standoffs with rubber clamps to leave glass undamaged.
- **Masonry and rendered exterior facades:** Retail plazas in Al Barsha, Deira, and Bur Dubai feature rendered masonry. Signs mounted to concrete use heavy expansion anchors or threaded studs. Our crew unbolts brackets and unseats anchor sleeves or cuts non-removable studs flush below the render line, preparing walls for fresh paint.
- **Interior shopping mall bulkheads:** Inside shopping malls, storefront fascias sit on gypsum bulkheads above glass entrances. Because drywall is fragile, our crew methodically cuts away silicone joints, backs out anchors, unclips raceways, and lowers components using padded access stands.

## What factors affect signage removal pricing?

We calculate commercial signage removal pricing with total upfront transparency, assessing the physical and logistical requirements of your specific installation:

- **Mounting height and access equipment:** Low-level ground-floor signage accessible by step ladder requires less rigging setup than double-height storefront signs or multi-storey exterior fascias requiring mobile scaffolding towers or boom lifts.
- **Sign dimensions and weight:** A compact acrylic reception logo plaque involves significantly less handling and haulage capacity than a heavy 10-metre illuminated steel lightbox.
- **Electrical wiring complexity:** Standard non-illuminated metal lettering unfastens quickly, whereas multi-circuit illuminated signs with internal transformers, capacitors, and conduit raceways require systematic electrical disconnection.
- **Facade material and anchoring type:** Simple surface screws on timber backing boards take less labour to release than high-tensile chemical anchors set into reinforced concrete or structural glass fittings.
- **Scheduling timeframe:** Standard daytime de-fits offer standard operational efficiency, whereas overnight mall de-fits require dedicated night-shift teams and coordinated loading dock bookings.

> **Tip:** Confirm that your facility electrician de-energises the primary electrical circuit supplying the signboard at your distribution board before our arrival. This ensures our team can begin unfastening brackets immediately without safety delays.

## Responsible disposal: recycling metals, acrylic, and electrical ballasts

Commercial signboards contain valuable, recyclable materials that should never be sent to landfills. Our team practices sustainable material recovery at every stage of the clearance process:

- **Aluminium and structural metals:** Lightbox frame extrusions, mounting raceways, and channel letter backs are sorted by alloy and delivered to licensed metal reclamation facilities in Al Quoz.
- **Acrylic and polycarbonate panels:** Translucent face panels and solid 3D lettering are segregated from metal backing plates and processed through commercial polymer recycling channels.
- **Copper wiring and electrical transformers:** Low-voltage wiring, ballast transformers, and LED drivers are channeled into certified electronic waste recovery streams.
- **Architectural glass and backing boards:** Standoff glass plaques and composite backing boards are sorted into appropriate municipal recycling streams.

By sorting components on site, we minimise landfill waste while complying with Dubai Municipality directives.
