## Industrial lease handovers and landlord de-fit requirements

Handing over an industrial warehouse shed in Dubai requires adherence to strict lease clauses and landlord dilapidation schedules:

### Flush slab grinding requirements

Most warehouse tenancy contracts across Al Quoz and Dubai Investments Park explicitly state that the tenant must return the concrete floor in an undamaged, smooth condition. When heavy machinery, car lifts, or structural workbenches are installed, contractors drill deep 16mm or 20mm anchor holes into the post-tensioned or reinforced floor slab. Simply knocking bolts loose with a sledgehammer shatters the surrounding concrete and results in costly landlord repair back-charges. Our technicians use high-torque cut-off saws to sever anchors at base level and grind the remaining studs perfectly flush with the concrete surface.

### Avoiding penalty holdover rents

Industrial leases in Dubai typically impose severe financial penalties if a tenant fails to vacate by the final lease expiration date. Incomplete de-fits or abandoned scrap can trigger holdover rent penalties amounting to double the normal daily rate. ## What factors affect the cost of a workshop clearance in Dubai

Every workshop cleanout involves unique logistical and material considerations. Upfront pricing is determined by:

- **Total volume and physical tonnage:** Industrial materials are dense. A fabrication shop packed with solid steel worktables represents high weight tonnage, influencing vehicle sizing, axle limits, and disposal logistics.
- **Dismantling and unbolting complexity:** Freestanding equipment that can be rolled directly onto our hydraulic tail-lift costs less to clear than heavy structural racking anchored 150mm into concrete that requires power grinding and overhead deconstruction.
- **Proportion of recyclable metals versus general debris:** Scrap steel, aluminium, and copper hold positive recovery value at licensed Al Quoz smelting facilities, which helps offset overall clearance labour costs compared to clearing low-value treated timber or drywall debris.
- **Unit access and loading conditions:** Ground-level roller shutter doors with wide driveway aprons permit rapid vehicle loading, whereas units with low headroom or restricted communal loading bays require additional manual handling.
