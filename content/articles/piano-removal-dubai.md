An old piano that nobody plays, an inherited upright with cracked keys, or a digital piano left behind at the end of a tenancy: **piano removal in Dubai** is one of the hardest single-item jobs to do yourself. Pianos are extremely heavy, top-heavy and awkward through doorways and lifts. This guide explains how disposal works, what to check before you book, how access affects the job, and how to get a clear price.

## How does piano removal in Dubai work?

**Send photos, agree the price, and our crew takes the piano out and away for responsible disposal.** Four steps:

1. **Send photos** of the piano, rough dimensions, your area, the floor and whether there's a service lift.
2. **Know the price before we start**, based on the piano type, size and access.
3. **We carry it out**, planning the route through doorways, corridors and the lift before lifting.
4. **Responsible disposal:** wood, metal and electronics are recycled whenever possible.

> **Note:** This is a disposal service for pianos you want gone for good. If you want a piano moved to a new home and kept in good playing condition, use a specialist piano moving company.

## Which pianos can be removed?

- **Upright pianos**, including older, heavier models
- **Digital pianos and electric keyboards**, with their stands and benches
- **Piano stools and benches**

**Grand and baby grand pianos** need specialist equipment to remove legs and lyre and move the body on its side. Tell us if yours is a grand, and we'll tell you honestly whether we can take it or whether a specialist is the better choice.

## Should you sell, give away or dispose of the piano?

**Check whether the piano has value before you book disposal.** Some older pianos are worth selling, while many aren't, because repairs and tuning cost more than the instrument is worth.

- **Ask a piano tuner or dealer** for an honest view of its condition.
- **Try a listing** if it's playable and in good shape.
- **Choose disposal** if it's broken, badly out of tune, damaged by humidity, or nobody wants it.

If the piano can't be sold and needs to go by a deadline, such as a move-out or handover, disposal is usually the quickest option.

## Why does access matter so much for a piano?

**Because a piano's weight and shape make the route the hardest part of the job.** Before collection, check:

- **Doorway widths** along the route, including the apartment door and any internal doors
- **Tight turns** in corridors and stairwells
- **Stairs:** how many flights, and whether there's a landing to turn on
- **The service lift:** its door width and depth, and whether it needs booking
- **Floors:** delicate flooring along the route, so it can be protected

In apartment towers in areas like [Downtown Dubai](/downtown-dubai/), check with your building management whether the service lift needs reserving and which loading bay the truck should use. In villas in areas like [Al Barsha](/al-barsha/), pianos are often on the ground floor, which makes the job simpler, but steps at the entrance still need planning.

> **Tip:** Measure the piano's height, width and depth, and photograph the route from the piano to the front door. It's the single most useful thing you can send.

## How do you prepare a piano for collection?

**Clear the route and empty the piano. The crew handles the lifting.** Before collection day:

1. **Empty the piano stool and the piano itself.** Remove sheet music, ornaments and anything stored inside the lid.
2. **Close and lock the lid** if it has a key, so the keyboard cover can't swing open while it's moved.
3. **Clear the route** from the piano to the front door: rugs, furniture, plants and anything fragile.
4. **Book the service lift** if your building requires it, and confirm the loading bay with building management.
5. **Unplug digital pianos** and coil the cables. Keep the power adapter with the piano.
6. **Tell your neighbours** if the corridor will be blocked briefly. It saves awkward moments on the day.

Don't try to move the piano yourself first, even a short distance. Pianos are top-heavy, castors on old uprights often seize, and tipping one can injure people and damage floors.

## Digital pianos and keyboards

**Digital pianos are a much lighter job than acoustic pianos, but they still need proper disposal.** A digital piano with a wooden cabinet can still be heavy and bulky, and it contains circuit boards, speakers and wiring. These count as electronic waste, so they go for appropriate recycling rather than general waste.

If you're replacing a digital piano or keyboard along with other electronics, such as old speakers, a TV or computers, they can all go in the same visit.
