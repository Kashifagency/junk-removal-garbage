## Eco-friendly glass recycling in Dubai

Glass is one of the most recyclable materials on earth, capable of being remelted indefinitely without loss of purity or structural integrity. However, when glass is tossed into mixed rubbish skips, it contaminates organic and paper waste streams, eventually ending up buried in municipal landfill sites.

Our team ensures that clean architectural and furniture glass collected across Dubai is diverted away from landfills:

- **Cullet separation:** Salvaged clear and tinted glass panels are delivered to licensed recycling partners in Al Quoz Industrial Area.
- **Crushing and processing:** The glass is mechanically crushed into cullet, sorted by colour, and cleansed of ceramic and metal contaminants.
- **Industrial reuse:** Processed glass cullet is utilized in manufacturing new glass containers, fiberglass insulation, and eco-friendly construction aggregates for road base stabilization.
