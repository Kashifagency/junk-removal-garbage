## Dubai high-rise logistics: elevator protection, narrow doors, and plumbing safety

Removing an awkwardly proportioned, heavy bathtub from a Dubai high-rise residential building presents distinct logistical challenges that require professional planning.

### Plumbing isolation and drain disconnection

Before our team unfastens or lifts any bathtub or sink, all water plumbing must be completely shut off and disconnected. Dubai building codes strictly require that main water stops or fixture angle valves be fully isolated, flexible supply lines unscrewed, and drain P-traps uncoupled by a qualified plumber before collection. Our removal crew handles the physical transport and haulage of dry fixtures, ensuring zero risk of water leaks or pipe damage in multi-storey apartment blocks.

### Tight bathroom doorways and corridor corners

Bathrooms in modern Dubai apartments are frequently designed with compact entry doorways (typically 65–70 cm wide). Carrying a 170 cm long bathtub through these narrow openings requires angling the tub onto its side using specialized moving straps. Our crew wraps the fixture edges in thick padded blankets to prevent gouging doorframes, cracking tile thresholds, or damaging hallway walls.

### Freight elevator booking and loading dock clearance

In luxury towers across areas like [Dubai Marina](/dubai-marina/) and [Downtown Dubai](/downtown-dubai/), building management mandates that all bulky construction items and sanitaryware travel exclusively via designated service elevators. We coordinate vehicle arrival times to coincide with your pre-booked elevator window and secure building security gate passes swiftly with our registered commercial credentials.
