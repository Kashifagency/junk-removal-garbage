## Majlis removal Dubai: how our dismantling process works

Our experienced garden clearance crew executes outdoor majlis dismantling through a methodical, multi-step process that safeguards your property:

1. **Send photos for straightforward pricing:** Take several clear photos of your outdoor majlis tent from the outside and inside, showing its approximate footprint (e.g., 4m × 6m or 5m × 8m), roof height, and pathway access to the street. Send these via WhatsApp to **+971 55 765 1866**. We review the structure and provide comprehensive, fixed pricing before scheduling your collection.
2. **Electrical and utility disconnection:** Before dismantling begins, the client must ensure that all electrical supply lines feeding the tent's chandelier lighting, perimeter wall sockets, and split air conditioning units are disconnected and safely capped by a qualified electrician.
3. **Interior clearance and textile stripping:** Our team begins inside the majlis, clearing out traditional low floor cushions (diwaniya seating), heavy wool or synthetic rugs, decorative curtains, and wall hangings. We then unclip the interior patterned fabric lining and thermal insulation fleece.
4. **Outer membrane and canvas de-lacing:** We safely de-tension and remove the outer weatherproof PVC membrane and heavy canvas roof coverings, folding and rolling the materials into manageable bundles for truck loading.
5. **Structural steel framework deconstruction:** Using impact drivers, spanners, and reciprocating metal saws where bolts are seized, our technicians unbolt the roof rafters, ridge purlins, and vertical support posts from top to bottom, preventing unexpected frame collapse.
6. **Subfloor uplift and anchor bolt extraction:** If the majlis sits on an elevated timber deck, we unbolt the wooden joists and marine plywood panels. Anchor bolts drilled into patio slabs are extracted or mechanically ground flush with the ground surface.
7. **Garden cleanup and licensed recycling:** We sweep up loose screws, clips, and debris, rake adjacent turf or gravel, and load all components onto our haulage trucks for compliant delivery to licensed material recovery plants in Al Quoz.

## Components of outdoor majlis structures we take down

A typical Dubai garden majlis comprises diverse materials that require careful separation during dismantling:

- **Structural steel and aluminium framework:** Heavy tubular steel columns, structural trusses, galvanised ridge beams, and corner brackets unbolted from the top ridge down.
- **Outer weatherproof covers:** High-density PVC-coated polyester membranes, canvas canopies, and side curtains rolled into bundles.
- **Thermal and acoustic insulation:** Multi-layered fiberglass batting, reflective foil barriers, and high-density foam panels.
- **Traditional interior fabric linings:** Red-and-black geometric Sadu fabric wall panels, decorative ceiling drapery, and acoustic curtains.
- **Flooring and decking elements:** Raised treated timber subfloor frames, composite decking tiles, marine plywood sheets, and synthetic carpeting.
- **Interior furnishings and accessories:** Traditional Arabic floor sofas, corner armrests, bolster cushions, low wooden tables, and brass lanterns.
- **Cooling ductwork and brackets:** Flexible insulated air conditioning ducts, metal louvers, wall mounting brackets, and condenser stands.

> **Important:** While our crew completely dismantles the physical majlis structure and takes down internal light fixtures, all primary 220V/380V electrical supply cables must be fully disconnected and isolated at the main distribution board by a qualified technician prior to our arrival.

## Seasonal temporary tents vs permanent steel-structure majlis

Understanding the construction style of your majlis helps determine the removal timeframe and equipment needed:

| Feature | Seasonal / Ramadan fabric tent | Permanent steel-frame outdoor majlis |
|---|---|---|
| **Typical frame** | Lightweight modular aluminium or thin-wall steel pipes | Heavy-duty structural steel I-beams or box sections |
| **Anchoring method** | Ground pegs, sandbags, or lightweight curb anchors | Heavy chemical anchor bolts set into concrete foundations |
| **Insulation** | Single or double-layer canvas; minimal insulation | Thick multi-layer thermal fleece and double PVC skins |
| **Dismantling duration** | 2 to 4 hours | 5 to 8 hours depending on dimensions and subfloor |
| **Lifting equipment** | Hand tools, ladders, and manual carrying | Heavy-duty power tools, staging ladders, and scaffolding |
| **Landscaping impact** | Minimal ground disturbance | Minor anchor hole patching or paver realignment |
| **Disposal streams** | Canvas recycling and light tubular metal scrap | Heavy structural steel smelting and timber deck recovery |

## What determines majlis dismantling and removal pricing?

We provide clear, transparent pricing confirmed before work commences. Our rates are calculated based on realistic operational factors:

- **Tent footprint and roof height:** Compact 3m × 4m tents take significantly less time to deconstruct than expansive 6m × 10m double-pitch pavilions that require working from tall safety ladders or aluminium access towers.
- **Framework engineering and connection type:** Modular pipe systems with push-button or simple clamp fittings dismantle rapidly. Heavy welded steel frames with structural cross-bracing require extensive mechanical cutting and unbolting.
- **Anchoring method and ground restoration:** Tents secured with surface ballasts or removable stakes involve minimal labour. Cabins anchored with high-tensile steel chemical anchors into reinforced concrete slabs require angle grinding and mortar hole-filling.
- **Interior furnishing and carpet volume:** A fully furnished majlis containing multiple rows of upholstered sofas, bulky floor mattresses, heavy carpets, and wall-mounted air conditioners requires more truck capacity and sorting time than an empty canvas shell.
- **Villa access distance:** Clearance jobs where our truck can park directly next to the garden side gate are faster to execute than properties requiring crew members to hand-carry 4-metre steel poles through long, stepped side corridors.

## Responsible disposal and recycling of tent materials in Dubai

At Shanan Junk Removal, sustainable waste management is fundamental to our service. Outdoor majlis structures yield high volumes of reusable and recyclable industrial raw materials:

- **Scrap steel recycling:** Structural steel purlins, upright posts, and base plates are segregated by grade and transported to certified metal recycling smelters in Al Quoz, where they are melted into secondary construction steel.
- **Aluminium reclamation:** Window frames, threshold trims, and extrusion tracks are cleaned of screws and sent to aluminium foundries for reprocessing.
- **Timber deck recycling:** Solid timber framing and composite deck boards are diverted to licensed wood processing facilities to be converted into industrial mulch or composite board feedstock.
- **Responsible textile management:** Heavy synthetic PVC canvases that have deteriorated under UV radiation are separated from metallic eyelets and delivered to municipal-approved waste management facilities in full compliance with [Law No. 18 of 2024 Regulating Waste Management](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2024/Law%20No.%20(18)%20of%202024%20Regulating%20Waste%20Management.html).
