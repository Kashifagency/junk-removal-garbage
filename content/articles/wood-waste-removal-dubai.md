## Contractor best practices: how to store and prepare wood waste on site

Implementing basic timber storage procedures on active construction and fit-out sites streamlines clearance operations, minimizes safety hazards, and lowers collection costs:

1. **Designate a dedicated, sheltered timber storage zone:** Establish a defined wood waste holding area away from active vehicle driveways and high-traffic pedestrian paths. Keeping timber covered with a heavy tarpaulin prevents sudden rain or intense summer sun from warping and degrading clean boards destined for recycling.
2. **Flatten boards and stack by approximate length:** Stacking flat plywood sheets together and grouping uniform 2x4 framing studs into aligned piles allows our crew to load vehicles rapidly, maximising available truck payload and reducing on-site labour time.
3. **De-nail timber studs where practical:** If site labourers have downtime, knocking down or extracting large framing nails with a pry bar significantly enhances safety and reduces bundle volume. While our team handles nail-embedded wood, pre-treating studs speeds up loading.
4. **Keep sawdust and fine shavings bagged:** Collect wood dust, planar shavings, and router debris into heavy woven polypropylene bags rather than leaving loose piles on concrete floors. This prevents dust from becoming airborne during high winds or air conditioning system testing.
5. **Separate chemically treated wood from clean timber:** Keep CCA pressure-treated timber or chemical-stained wood separate from untreated natural pine and hardwoods. Informing our team in advance ensures materials are routed to the proper licensed processing stream.

## Dubai Municipality Circular 12-10-1 and on-site wood segregation

Disposing of construction timber in Dubai is closely governed by official municipal environmental directives. Contractors and property developers must adhere to strict segregation protocols to maintain compliant operations:

### The mandatory segregation directive (Circular 12-10-1)

Under [Dubai Municipality Circular 12-10-1](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2024/12/12-10-1.pdf) issued to engineering consultants and construction contractors, all construction and demolition waste must be segregated on site into distinct, barriered material streams. Wood and timber waste must be separated from concrete masonry, rebar steel, tiles, and plastics. Contractors are forbidden from loading co-mingled debris into single transport containers. Our specialised wood waste collection ensures that your timber stream is hauled independently to licensed wood recovery depots, keeping your project fully aligned with municipal engineering audits.

### Penalties for illegal dumping under Law No. 18 of 2024

Throwing scrap wood, splintered formwork, or nail-studded framing into communal street bins, open desert plots, or roadside alleys violates Dubai cleanliness regulations. Under [Executive Council Resolution No. 14 of 2015](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2015/Executive%20Council%20Resolution%20No.%20(14)%20of%202015.html) and [Law No. 18 of 2024](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2024/Law%20No.%20(18)%20of%202024%20Regulating%20Waste%20Management.html) Regulating Waste Management, illegal disposal of commercial or construction waste carries severe municipal fines starting from AED 500 up to AED 10,000, with mandatory remediation costs charged to the offending party. Engaging our licensed haulage service eliminates legal exposure and guarantees lawful disposal.
