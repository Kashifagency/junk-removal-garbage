## Eco-friendly rubber recycling in Dubai Municipality depots

Disposing of synthetic and natural rubber products in Dubai is governed by environmental directives aimed at eliminating tire and polymer dumping:

### Banning rubber waste from municipal landfills

Under Dubai Municipality Law No. 18 of 2024 Regulating Waste Management, commercial operators are strictly prohibited from dumping rubber flooring or bulk polymers into general waste compactors. Non-compliant dumping incurs substantial commercial fines. Our team adheres to mandatory on-site segregation, keeping clean rubber tiles separate from general packaging or timber debris.

### Crumb rubber reclamation in Al Quoz

Rubber gym flooring is an exceptional candidate for secondary material recovery. All uplifted rubber tiles and rolls collected by our crews are transported to licensed recycling facilities in Al Quoz and Jebel Ali. At these specialist plants, the vulcanized rubber is mechanically cleaned, stripped of metal fasteners, and cryogenically milled into fine crumb rubber. This recycled granulate is subsequently repurposed to manufacture rubber playground pavers, equestrian arena footing, and asphalt rubber modifiers for regional road networks.
