## Commercial and industrial logistics: safety, gate passes, and DCD compliance

Executing scrap metal clearances across Dubai's industrial free zones and commercial districts requires rigorous operational coordination, safety management, and regulatory compliance.

### Dubai Civil Defence compliance and fire hazard reduction

Under Dubai Civil Defence (DCD) industrial fire prevention codes, factory and warehouse yards must maintain completely clear perimeter pathways and unhindered access to external fire hydrants and hose reels. Discarded scrap metal, metal barrels, and tangled rebar dumped in service yards not only present severe physical trip hazards but also block emergency evacuation routes, leading to immediate fines during municipal or DCD inspections. Our crew clears outdoor compounds rapidly, restoring compliant emergency access across your facility.

### On-site cold cutting versus hot work permits

Sectioning heavy steel girders or metal storage tanks with oxy-acetylene cutting torches requires obtaining formal Hot Work Permits from industrial park authorities, establishing dedicated fire watch teams, and setting up spark barrier enclosures. To avoid costly permit delays and eliminate fire risks, our crews utilise powerful brushless reciprocating saws and hydraulic cold-cutting shears. This cold-cutting methodology produces zero open sparks, eliminates heat transfer to adjacent structures, and allows us to section oversized metals safely without interrupting your facility's operations.

### Industrial gate passes, NOCs, and loading bay protocols

Operating within master industrial parks such as Jebel Ali Free Zone (JAFZA), Dubai Investments Park (DIP), and Dubai Industrial City demands strict security compliance. Our transport vehicles and personnel carry all required commercial trade licences, vehicle registrations, and UAE safety credentials needed to secure gate passes swiftly. We coordinate closely with your warehouse facility manager to schedule loading during designated off-peak hours, preventing disruption to active shipping bays and forklift traffic.
