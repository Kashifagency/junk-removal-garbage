## Legal considerations: Protecting landlords during cleanouts

In Dubai, rental relationships and dispute resolutions are governed by comprehensive statutory frameworks, notably [Law No. 18 of 2024 Regulating Waste Management](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2024/Law%20No.%20(18)%20of%202024%20Regulating%20Waste%20Management.html) and Dubai tenancy legislation administered by the Dubai Land Department and the Rental Disputes Center.

Landlords must adhere to the following best practices before clearing tenant belongings:

1. **Ensure formal court execution:** Never attempt to clear a tenant's belongings based solely on rent arrears or unanswered phone calls. The Rental Disputes Center must issue a formal eviction execution order, and a court bailiff must execute legal possession.
2. **Conduct an initial photographic inventory:** Before our crew arrives, walk through the property with your leasing agent and photograph all abandoned items. Keep an archive of this visual record in your tenancy dispute file.
3. **Check for official documents:** While our team is instructed to set aside official papers, landlords should inspect drawers and desk spaces personally for passports, visas, Emirates IDs, marriage certificates, or business contracts.
4. **Authorize disposal in writing:** Once you are satisfied that no exempt personal property remains, you authorise our team to haul away the remaining furniture, bedding, appliances, and debris for recycling and licensed disposal.

By following these procedural steps, property owners protect themselves against unwarranted civil claims while expediting property turnover.

## Why avoid leaving eviction debris in public areas or municipal bins?

Faced with a property full of rubbish, some property managers consider dragging old mattresses and broken sofas to the nearest community bin enclosure or street curb. In Dubai, this practice is strictly prohibited.

Under [Executive Council Resolution No. 14 of 2015](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2015/Executive%20Council%20Resolution%20No.%20(14)%20of%202015.html), dumping bulky waste, broken furniture, or commercial rubbish in public open areas, empty plots, or beside municipal street bins carries immediate fines starting from AED 500 up to AED 1,000 for residential violations, escalating dramatically for repeat or commercial offences. Municipal inspectors patrol community bin zones and trace discarded items back to unit owners via packaging labels and CCTV footage.

Our private service guarantees compliant hauling: every piece of furniture, broken appliance, and bag of rubbish is loaded into our enclosed vehicle and taken to approved transfer stations, safeguarding you against municipal fines.
