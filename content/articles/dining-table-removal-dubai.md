## High-rise apartment logistics: navigating Dubai towers

Removing heavy furniture from modern Dubai apartment towers requires careful navigation of building management regulations and physical architectural constraints.

### Service elevators and protective elevator padding

Residential buildings in Dubai Marina, Downtown Dubai, and Business Bay have strict facility management rules regarding the transport of bulky goods. Standard passenger elevators are strictly off-limits for heavy furniture moving. Building management requires the reservation of a dedicated service elevator, which must be lined with heavy protective canvas padding to prevent wall gouging. Our team coordinates with building security protocols, ensuring removals take place during approved contractor moving hours (typically 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on weekdays).

### Moving permits and gate passes

Most master-developer communities and tower management associations require tenants to submit a move-out or bulky item removal permit prior to granting contractor access. We supply all necessary vehicle and crew documentation to help you secure your security gate pass without delays on moving day.

### Protecting delicate apartment interiors

Navigating an 8-seater solid wood table or a 2.4-metre marble slab through narrow apartment hallways requires technical finesse. Our crews use protective furniture blankets, neoprene floor runners, and corner guards to safeguard polished porcelain tiles, hardwood parquet, and fresh wall paint from accidental scrapes and scuffs.

## Dubai Municipality free pickup vs private dining table disposal

Dubai Municipality offers a free bulky waste pickup service for domestic residents via WhatsApp at 800900. While valuable for eligible suburban villas, it has clear limitations for dining furniture:

- Dubai Municipality **does not collect from high-rise apartment towers**; residents are instructed to place items curbside at ground level.
- Municipal crews **do not enter residences, dismantle furniture, or unbolt heavy stone pedestals**.
- Many freehold and master-developer communities (including Downtown Dubai, Palm Jumeirah, and Marina) fall outside the municipal collection zone.
- Waiting times for municipal appointments can stretch several days, creating serious problems if your lease expires tomorrow.
